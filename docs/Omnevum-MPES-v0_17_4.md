# 0. Purpose, authority, and operating invariant

## 0.1 Purpose

Omnevum is a personal Life OS for understanding, operating, and improving the relevant parts of life as an interconnected whole without pretending that a person is fully measurable, controllable, or optimizable.

The product shall help the user answer, when useful:

- What matters now?
- What changed?
- What is stale, uncertain, or contradicted?
- What depends on what?
- Where is a local optimization harming the larger system?
- Where could one intervention improve several outcomes?
- What assumption or bottleneck dominates?
- What should stop, continue, change, or be reviewed?
- What evidence would change the conclusion?

The product shall preserve user agency, privacy, portability, recoverability, plural values, ambiguity, and the legitimacy of leaving some areas untracked or deliberately unoptimized.

## 0.2 Stand-alone sufficiency

This MPES is intended to be sufficient for a capable senior AI engineer to carry Omnevum from current draft through discovery, research, architecture, implementation, qualification, release, operation, maintenance, update, deprecation, retirement, and archival without requiring the original chat history.

The repository and durable engineering state shall outrank conversational memory. A fresh qualified AI engineer shall be able to reconstruct the accepted project state from the repository, this MPES, machine-readable control artifacts, tests, evidence, provenance, and declared credentials/access.

OMN-GOV-013: Every substantive project, package, module, workflow, automation, generated specification, report, export profile/schema, artifact class, app/game, or other product/system component created or materially revised under this MPES is a governed descendant. At creation or material revision, its native durable contract SHALL carry the smallest sufficient applicable Omnevum/Omni obligations needed for independent correct operation and lifecycle without relying on this conversation or an unavailable ancestor artifact. This includes, where applicable, truth/provenance, canonical ownership, authority, privacy/security, accessibility, source/currentness, verification, idempotence, update/invalidation, recovery, versioning, portability, maintenance, retirement, and onward inheritance. A descendant that can itself create or materially revise governed descendants SHALL impose the same applicable semantics recursively with no fixed generation-depth limit. This is semantic inheritance, not prose copying: inapplicable mechanisms are omitted; external dependencies are validated rather than rewritten; and a medium unable to carry a behavior itself SHALL enforce the nearest generation, validation, execution, update, repair, or invalidation boundary.

## 0.3 Authority order

When sources conflict, apply this order:

1. applicable law, rights, host/platform constraints, and actual external authority;
2. latest explicit owner direction for Omnevum;
3. this MPES and its accepted versioned amendments;
4. accepted requirements, ADRs, threat models, acceptance profiles, and baselines derived under this MPES;
5. observed repository/runtime/target truth;
6. derived summaries, models, AI output, generated projections, and historical notes.

Implementation may disprove compliance but cannot redefine the requirement that it violates.

## 0.4 Standing owner delegation and autonomous execution

The owner explicitly delegates maximum ordinary project-scoped engineering authority to the AI engineering system.

For the purpose of completing Omnevum, the AI engineer SHALL presume that it is authorized to perform all ordinary reversible or safely recoverable development actions necessary for completion, including:

- inspect, create, edit, move, refactor, and delete project files;
- create branches, worktrees, commits, pull requests, issues, release candidates, and repository metadata when available;
- select, install, update, pin, replace, remove, or wrap development and runtime dependencies after license/security/currentness review;
- research the Web, standards, browser behavior, vendor APIs, FOSS projects, upstream source, best practices, security guidance, accessibility guidance, and comparable products;
- run builds, tests, linters, static analysis, accessibility tools, security tools, profilers, fuzzers, simulators, browser automation, and target-device testing;
- create or modify CI/CD, build, release, provenance, SBOM, signing/attestation, Pages/static-host deployment, and recovery tooling within project-owned targets;
- create and use local or remote test environments, mock services, emulators, sandboxes, disposable storage, and synthetic data;
- create specialist subagents and parallel workers when they provide net positive leverage;
- choose models, tools, methods, architectures, algorithms, libraries, and FOSS dependencies using current evidence and professional judgment;
- implement all product, platform, app/game framework, migration, localization, documentation, testing, security, recovery, and lifecycle work needed for accepted completion;
- continue iterating, debugging, repairing, replacing failed approaches, and re-running evidence until the accepted completion criteria are met.

The phrase "maximum autonomy" and the owner's instruction to proceed "by any means possible" mean: exhaust creative, efficient, lawful, safe, technically legitimate, and project-authorized routes before declaring a blocker. They do NOT authorize bypassing actual host/provider controls, violating law or third-party rights, falsifying evidence, weakening gates to obtain PASS, fabricating credentials, evading security controls, or silently expanding access beyond what an external system actually grants.

OMN-GOV-001: Within the accepted Omnevum project scope, the AI engineering system SHALL presume that all ordinary project permissions, authorizations, repository rights, tool access, network access, development credentials/references, test targets, subagent authority, and reversible engineering powers necessary to complete the work are available and authorized unless actual environment/tool evidence proves a specific capability absent or externally gated. It SHALL attempt the available path and inspect the environment before asking the owner. This standing presumption prevents needless permission-seeking; it does not fabricate an external credential, override an enforced provider/host boundary, waive law/rights, or convert an unavailable effect into an executed one.

OMN-GOV-002: The AI engineering system MAY bootstrap, install, configure, replace, or generate non-secret project-local development infrastructure, harnesses, test fixtures, adapters, and automation without owner confirmation when doing so is reversible, within project scope, and improves time-to-accepted-result. It MAY spawn and coordinate specialist subagents/parallel workers without asking the owner when the parent scope and actual host capability permit it.

If an external service presents an actually non-delegable gate that cannot be completed by the AI despite presumed project authority, the AI shall first seek a legitimate architecture/workflow alternative that avoids the gate without weakening the accepted outcome. If none exists, it shall complete all independent work, prepare the exact final action required, and defer only that irreducible step. No routine implementation question, approval, "continue" prompt, or manual command transcription shall be requested from the human.

## 0.5 Human-effort and justified-autonomy contract

Both the engineering path and the ordinary product runtime shall be optimized for near-zero routine human effort and minimum necessary attention.

The AI engineer shall NOT ask the human to:

- choose ordinary implementation details;
- choose among equally acceptable libraries without a product-level value tradeoff;
- debug code;
- repair tests;
- run commands that the AI can run;
- copy information already available to the project;
- manually coordinate CI or releases;
- repeatedly approve continuation;
- perform ordinary documentation, migration, dependency, packaging, or deployment work.

The human is expected to return when Omnevum has reached the accepted completed-product gate, except where an external system, law, irreversible consequence, physical-device action, or genuinely subjective owner decision makes earlier human action unavoidable.

OMN-GOV-007: The product runtime SHALL optimize for maximum justified autonomy and minimum human attention. For routine admitted work it SHALL prefer `infer -> retrieve -> derive -> cross-check -> apply a scoped confirmed rule -> make a safe reversible/provisional decision -> surface only a material exception` before asking the user. Automation SHALL preserve truth, agency, authority, privacy, reversibility, and domain-specific acceptance gates; an inability to prove a high-consequence interpretation is not permission to guess.

OMN-GOV-008: Ordinary mechanical processing SHALL be exception-driven. When deterministic invariants, source evidence, prior confirmed scoped rules, and confidence/impact gates make an outcome safe, Omnevum SHOULD complete it without requiring review. User attention SHALL be requested only when an unresolved ambiguity, value judgment, external/irreversible effect, authority boundary, or materially consequential uncertainty cannot be safely represented as unknown, provisional, or scenario-based.

OMN-GOV-009: A user correction or confirmed disposition MAY create or refine a reusable scoped rule, parser profile, alias, classification, reconciliation recipe, or automation preference so the same routine work does not repeatedly return to the user. Learned behavior SHALL remain inspectable, reversible, versioned where material, provenance-bound, narrowly scoped enough to avoid unsafe generalization, and invalidated when source format, semantics, authority, or evidence materially changes.

OMN-GOV-010: Omnevum SHALL minimize human data entry. Before asking the user to type, copy, classify, reconcile, confirm, or re-enter information, the owning workflow SHALL first reuse already-authorized canonical data, source artifacts, prior confirmed values/rules, structured imports, extraction/OCR, Device/Input capture, relationships, deterministic derivation, and safe defaults where applicable. Information already present or safely derivable SHALL NOT require duplicate manual entry merely because another module, view, workflow, or reporting surface consumes it. The preferred pattern is capture/import once, preserve provenance, and reuse by reference or derivation everywhere authorized.

OMN-GOV-011: Accepted source changes, corrections, classifications, reconciliations, and user decisions SHALL propagate automatically to materially dependent derived state, projections, summaries, forecasts, goals, views, alerts, and review status, or explicitly invalidate them when automatic recomputation is unsafe. Omnevum SHALL NOT require the user to manually synchronize the same meaning across multiple modules or repeatedly maintain derived copies.

OMN-GOV-012: Human work is a product cost and SHALL be measured proportionately in representative acceptance workflows. Where two designs satisfy the same truth, safety, privacy, authority, recovery, and usability requirements, prefer the one with fewer required user fields, repeated confirmations, navigation steps, exception reviews, maintenance actions, and manual reconciliation steps. A release SHALL NOT knowingly regress ordinary human burden without a documented material benefit or stronger controlling requirement.

## 0.6 Normative language

- SHALL / MUST: mandatory.
- SHALL NOT / MUST NOT: prohibited.
- SHOULD: preferred unless a documented stronger whole-system reason exists.
- MAY: permitted.
- NOT_APPLICABLE: requires explicit applicability rationale where the requirement family is otherwise expected.
- UNKNOWN: not evidence of failure or success; resolve or narrow the claim.

## 0.7 Quality priority

When all desirable properties cannot be simultaneously maximized, prefer:

1. law, rights, safety, canonical data integrity;
2. truth/correctness and recoverability;
3. security/privacy;
4. user agency/accessibility;
5. intended outcome and whole-system value;
6. simplicity and cognitive burden;
7. reliability/resilience;
8. performance/resource stewardship;
9. maintainability/replaceability;
10. extensibility;
11. cosmetic novelty.

## 0.8 Irreducibility, DRY, and Via Negativa

Permanent complexity must earn its lifecycle cost.

Before adding a subsystem, abstraction, framework, service, dependency, database, event bus, state store, AI route, notification system, permission system, sync mechanism, or module-specific utility, ask whether the requirement can be satisfied by:

1. no software / a simpler process;
2. the browser/OS;
3. an existing Omnevum capability;
4. an open interoperable standard;
5. mature maintained FOSS;
6. a narrow adapter/composition;
7. a small first-party extension;
8. only then, a new first-party subsystem.

Duplicate state or command authority is a defect unless independence is intentional and tested.

## 0.9 CONVERGE

OMN-GOV-003: Every normative SHALL/MUST/SHALL NOT/MUST NOT statement in this MPES is binding whether or not it already carries a hand-authored requirement ID. Before material implementation, the durable engineering controller SHALL produce and maintain a machine-readable Requirement Register that assigns a stable machine identity/source locator to every normative obligation, maps accepted IDs where present, and prevents unnumbered prose from falling outside traceability. Generated IDs/register rows are projections of this MPES and cannot rewrite its meaning.

Material releases and major specification revisions shall perform finite recursive closure over the material dependency graph:

1. establish exact candidate identity and source/dependency/toolchain state;
2. inspect for correction, necessary addition, deletion, merge/generalization, lossless compression, interface failure, source drift, privacy/security/recovery gaps, and structural simplification;
3. apply only net-positive changes and rerun affected evidence;
4. perform a complete zero-change pass;
5. perform a fresh zero-change confirmation using materially different evidence/evaluation where practical;
6. record a minimal external convergence receipt when reliable persistence exists.

A PASS label, unchanged file, version string, or prior conclusion is not convergence evidence.
## 0.10 XY-problem and outcome-first analysis

OMN-GOV-004: For every material owner request, proposed feature, inherited upstream behavior, FOSS capability, architecture mechanism, or implementation prescription, the engineering system SHALL distinguish the stated solution/request ("Y") from the underlying legitimate outcome/problem ("X") before committing irreversible architecture or broad implementation.

The XY analysis SHALL, proportionate to consequence and complexity:

- preserve the owner's stated request and constraints rather than silently substituting a different objective;
- identify the intended user outcome, failure being avoided, or capability gap;
- inspect whether the stated mechanism is necessary, merely one possible route, or a symptom of a deeper architectural problem;
- search existing Omnevum capabilities, browser/platform primitives, open standards, owner code, FOSS, integrations, and simpler compositions before creating new machinery;
- prefer the smallest solution that satisfies the actual outcome without losing material requested value;
- retain the stated solution when it remains the best whole-lifecycle route after analysis;
- record any material substitution and the evidence/rationale that makes it outcome-equivalent or superior;
- avoid turning ordinary reversible technical judgment into a human approval gate.

OMN-GOV-005: "Capability superset", "feature-rich", "maximum reuse", and "fastest completion" SHALL be interpreted as outcome constraints, not invitations to maximize feature count, dependency count, code volume, or architectural layers. The target is maximum justified user capability and ecosystem leverage per unit of complexity, risk, maintenance burden, and time-to-accepted-result.

OMN-GOV-006: A material request that is better satisfied by integration, import/export compatibility, shared capability generalization, or a simpler workflow SHALL NOT force duplication of an external commodity system merely to preserve superficial feature parity.

For trivial low-risk work, this analysis may remain implicit. For material architecture, major capability acquisition, or a proposed rejection/defer decision, the durable SDD/decision record SHOULD preserve the outcome, considered routes, selected route, and why it is not an XY mistake.

# 1. Product constitution

## 1.1 Product thesis

Omnevum is not a collection of isolated trackers. It is one personal operating environment in which life domains remain semantically distinct but become more useful through shared capabilities, relationships, evidence, time, and cross-domain synthesis.

The operating model is:

- semantic records = information;
- canonical owners = authoritative mutable meaning;
- capabilities = reusable things the platform can do;
- lenses = user-facing domain perspectives;
- surfaces = primary user entry points;
- projections = derived views across authorized records;
- intents/commands = bounded requests to act;
- brokers/policy = control of high-authority resources, egress, credentials, and effects;
- connectors = optional adapters to external systems;
- AI = optional shared intelligence, never authority by itself.

## 1.2 Human-centered boundary

OMN-PRD-001: The user is the authority-bearing person, not an engineered component owned by Omnevum.

OMN-PRD-002: Omnevum SHALL NOT require complete life capture, continuous monitoring, exhaustive goals, universal quantification, forced optimization, or resolution of every inconsistency.

OMN-PRD-003: Missing, qualitative, contradictory, intentionally excluded, or intentionally unoptimized information SHALL remain legitimate states.

OMN-PRD-004: Slack, redundancy, reversibility, optionality, exploration, leisure, and deliberate local suboptimization MAY be legitimate contributors to resilience and user value.

OMN-PRD-005: Other people SHALL remain independent stakeholders with their own rights, consent, uncertainty, and authority. Omnevum SHALL NOT treat them as optimization resources, hidden scores, predicted compliance, or manipulation targets.

OMN-PRD-006: Omnevum SHALL NOT construct hidden universal psychological, relationship, personality, engagement, sentiment, compliance, or social-scoring profiles.

OMN-PRD-007: Wellbeing, agency, dignity, legitimate values, essential needs, and sustainable capacity outrank optimization.

OMN-PRD-008: Home, Assistant, notifications, reviews, onboarding, and other first-party UX SHALL NOT use deception, fabricated urgency/scarcity, emotional pressure, dark patterns, or unsupported anthropomorphic dependency cues.

## 1.3 Non-goals

Omnevum is not:

- a desktop visual clone;
- a web desktop whose purpose is window novelty;
- a replacement for every commodity device app;
- a mandatory cloud/account platform;
- a universal writable life database;
- a hidden scoring/surveillance product;
- a task/calendar/feed engagement product;
- a mandatory AI product;
- an arbitrary same-origin JavaScript plugin marketplace in baseline releases;
- a self-modifying production runtime;
- a claim that browser storage survives device/origin loss without backup;
- a regulated medical, legal, tax, investment, or clinical authority merely by combining personal data.

Commodity email, calendar, maps, weather, office editing, password management, file management, device management, and similar functions SHOULD remain host/FOSS-owned unless Omnevum has a distinct semantic outcome that earns ownership.
## 1.4 Coherent capability-superset objective

OMN-PRD-009: Omnevum SHALL aim to become the coherent capability superset of the materially relevant owner-project and FOSS reference ecosystem as far as reasonably useful, technically feasible, rights-compliant, and whole-lifecycle positive under Pareto and Via Negativa.

"Capability superset" means that every materially relevant high-value capability discovered in the accepted reference corpus receives an explicit Omnevum disposition and that the resulting user outcome is available through one of:

- native Omnevum capability;
- Omnevum-native domain module;
- generalized shared capability that subsumes the narrower feature;
- qualified external integration/connector;
- qualified import/export/acquisition path;
- qualified compatibility layer;
- explicit defer/reject decision with current evidence.

It does NOT mean:

- line-for-line or screen-for-screen cloning;
- bundling every referenced application;
- preserving redundant frameworks or server stacks;
- importing desktop/native assumptions into the static-PWA core;
- keeping multiple implementations of the same shared mechanic merely because different upstreams contain them;
- delaying an independently useful MVP until every reference project has been mined.

OMN-PRD-010: A capability that is high user-value, low integration/lifecycle cost, compatible with the product boundary, and already proven by mature reusable FOSS has a presumption of inclusion. Deferral requires a concrete dependency, risk, sequencing, rights, platform, or opportunity-cost reason rather than lack of implementation convenience.

OMN-PRD-011: The engineering system SHALL maintain a capability-coverage view over the accepted reference corpus. Coverage SHALL be evaluated by user outcome and semantic breadth, not raw feature count. A broader Omnevum capability may satisfy multiple narrower upstream features; a qualified external integration may satisfy a commodity capability that Omnevum should not own.

OMN-PRD-012: Omnevum SHALL optimize along a moving Pareto frontier of useful breadth, coherence, development speed, maintainability, security/privacy, resource cost, updateability, and user burden. An addition dominated by an existing composition or by a simpler higher-leverage route is a Via-Negativa candidate even if it increases nominal feature count.

The initial release path therefore separates:

- MVP capability nucleus: the smallest vertically complete daily-usable system that proves the final architecture;
- continuous harvest queue: valuable capabilities/code/tests/semantics discovered from owner projects and FOSS and integrated in parallel where dependency-ready;
- accepted-version capability closure: the finite, date/version-bounded set of capabilities required for that release;
- ongoing ecosystem evolution: later upstream/new-entrant improvements handled through maintenance rather than making completion non-terminating.

# 2. System context and life model

## 2.1 System-of-systems model

The relevant whole may include:

- the user;
- other stakeholders;
- needs, values, principles, goals, and outcomes;
- evidence, knowledge, and uncertainty;
- time, capacity, money, assets, obligations, and reserves;
- health, fitness, reflection, learning, household, work, relationships, travel, and environment;
- external institutions and services;
- projects, decisions, interventions, constraints, risks, dependencies, and feedback.

OMN-SEM-001: The user SHALL control the boundary of cross-domain reasoning and MAY exclude any domain or record without the exclusion being treated as failure.

OMN-SEM-002: Material decisions and interventions SHALL consider affected stakeholders, time horizons, enabling systems, dependencies, interfaces, risks, uncertainty, and downstream effects proportionate to consequence.

OMN-SEM-003: Models remain representations. A graph edge, temporal sequence, simulation, AI narrative, or metric movement SHALL NOT alone establish causation.

OMN-SEM-004: Causal and synergy claims SHALL distinguish correlation, sequence, hypothesis, modeled mechanism, simulation, observation, intervention evidence, validation, and sustained benefit.

## 2.2 Default life lenses

The default distribution SHOULD include these customizable user-facing lenses:

- Direction
- People
- Self
- Resources
- Work
- Environment
- Knowledge
- Change

These are presentation templates, not permanent ontology, package, storage, or database boundaries.

Direction may cover purpose, values, principles, needs, constraints, assumptions, desired outcomes, measures of effectiveness, tradeoff guardrails, and review triggers.

People may cover relationships, shared history, commitments, important moments/interests, stakeholder roles, and user-authored relationship notes without hidden sentiment/compliance scoring.

Self may cover reflection, experiments, routines, habits, fitness protocols, focus/energy observations, and self-management records. Clinical authority is out of scope unless separately admitted and governed.

Resources may cover money, time, capacity, assets, housing, obligations, reserves, and material resources.

Work may cover roles, outcomes, projects, stakeholders, decisions, risks/issues, evidence, reviews, accomplishments, and development.

Environment may cover home, household dependencies, inventory/assets, maintenance, geography, commute, institutions, services, travel context, vehicles, technology dependencies, and important environmental conditions.

Knowledge may cover sources, claims, references, lessons, uncertainty, research collections, concepts, evidence relationships, and personal knowledge artifacts.

Change may cover goals, initiatives, projects, experiments, interventions, milestones, dependencies, capacity assumptions, benefit hypotheses, and stop/defer/review gates.

OMN-SEM-005: Users SHALL be able to rename, hide, merge, reorganize, or create lenses without changing stable record identities or shared semantic meaning.

OMN-SEM-006: Decisions and Reviews SHALL be cross-lens workspaces owned by the relevant domain/change context rather than a universal writable silo.

## 2.3 Intervention and learning model

Omnevum SHOULD support an Intervention concept with, where applicable:

- intended change;
- baseline;
- expected outcomes;
- affected domains/stakeholders;
- assumptions and evidence;
- costs and constraints;
- risks and reversibility;
- observation plan;
- review trigger;
- observed outcome;
- side effects/externalities;
- benefit validation;
- stop/continue/change decision.

This is intended to make Omnevum a personal learning system rather than merely a tracker.

## 2.4 Whole-system relationship, resource, and synergy model

OMN-SEM-007: Subject to the user's active Space/Scope, privacy, consent, and domain boundaries, Omnevum SHALL treat materially related records as parts of one relevant system rather than isolated tracker rows. A canonical fact, resource, obligation, goal, plan, risk, decision, intervention, or source MAY participate in many authorized projections through typed relationships while retaining one canonical owner and identity.

OMN-SEM-008: The relationship fabric SHALL support explicit typed semantics sufficient to represent material structures such as `depends_on`, `contributes_to`, `funds`, `consumes`, `earmarked_for`, `competes_for`, `constrains`, `enables`, `offsets`, `conflicts_with`, `supports`, `affected_by`, `derived_from`, and `measured_by`, or equivalent domain-specific types. Relationship types SHALL define direction, subject/object type constraints, provenance, confidence/truth class where derived, and whether the relationship is exclusive, additive, conditional, temporal, or merely informational when that distinction affects reasoning.

OMN-SEM-009: Omnevum SHOULD discover useful cross-domain relationships automatically from already-authorized evidence and deterministic semantics. High-confidence mechanically established links MAY be created automatically when reversible and non-consequential; uncertain or value-laden links SHALL remain proposals/hypotheses or explicit UNKNOWN rather than forcing the user to manually connect obvious records or silently inventing relationships. Confirmed relationship corrections SHOULD become reusable scoped rules under OMN-GOV-009.

OMN-SEM-010: A material change to canonical meaning SHALL trigger dependency-aware recomputation or invalidation of every materially affected authorized projection. Where relevant, one change SHOULD update dependent goals, plans, budgets, forecasts, risks, scenarios, attention items, reviews, and decision inputs without manual cross-module maintenance. The propagation graph SHALL be inspectable enough to answer `what changed because of this?` and `what depends on this?`.

OMN-SEM-011: Shared resources such as money, time, capacity, assets, space, inventory, and attention SHALL use explicit allocation semantics where simultaneous claims can conflict. Exclusive/consumptive allocations SHALL not be double-counted across goals or plans; non-exclusive enabling relationships MAY support several outcomes simultaneously when that is semantically valid. The system SHALL distinguish a resource being merely relevant to several outcomes from that resource being simultaneously committed to all of them.

OMN-SEM-012: Omnevum SHOULD search for cross-domain synergies and conflicts at the system level. It MAY surface an intervention, allocation, schedule, purchase, habit, decision, or plan that plausibly advances several authorized outcomes or removes a shared bottleneck, and it SHOULD identify when one local optimization harms another material outcome. Such synergy/conflict output SHALL distinguish observed evidence, deterministic consequence, modeled effect, hypothesis, and user preference; it SHALL preserve Pareto tradeoffs and SHALL NOT silently optimize the user's life to one scalar score.

OMN-SEM-013: Dependency propagation SHALL be cycle-safe and scenario-safe. Ordinary derived dependency traversal SHALL detect cycles and SHALL NOT recurse, oscillate, or repeatedly mutate canonical state merely because records refer to one another. A genuine feedback loop MAY be evaluated only inside an explicit Model/Intervention contract that defines the state variables, time/iteration semantics, governing assumptions, update/solver rule, convergence or termination criteria, failure/non-convergence behavior, sensitivity/uncertainty, and scenario identity. Scenario/hypothetical edges and values SHALL remain scoped derived state and SHALL NOT silently mutate adopted plans or baseline canonical relationships. An unintended or semantically undefined cycle SHALL surface as a model/relationship defect or unresolved condition rather than being auto-resolved by arbitrary ordering.


## 2.5 Finance / Resources domain specialization

Finance is a first-party Resources specialization over shared Omnevum capabilities, not a separate financial application, parallel database, or privileged authority. Its normal outcome is a self-maintaining personal financial model driven primarily by user-supplied statements/exports and requiring human attention only for material exceptions or value judgments.

OMN-FIN-001: Core Finance SHALL operate completely without a bank/broker credential, live financial-institution connection, or Omnevum financial backend. User-selected statements, exports, records, and manual corrections are the baseline acquisition path. The product SHALL NOT require the user to connect an account merely to obtain reporting, reconciliation, budgeting, forecasting, goals, anomaly review, or other core finance outcomes. A future connector MAY be separately admitted, but its absence SHALL not reduce this core contract.

OMN-FIN-029: Financial source files SHALL be the primary ordinary data-entry mechanism. Finance SHALL extract and reuse all supportable account identity, statement period, balance, transaction, counterparty, amount, currency, recurring-flow, debt, holding, income, fee, and related fields from supplied evidence rather than asking the user to transcribe them. Manual financial entry SHOULD be limited to genuinely unavailable source facts, user-authored goals/preferences/assumptions, and material corrections or unresolved exceptions. The normal user loop SHOULD be `provide/drop sources -> automatic processing and reconciliation -> review only material exceptions -> consume the updated analysis`.

OMN-FIN-030: A finance correction or accepted interpretation SHALL update the authoritative financial meaning once and automatically recompute or invalidate every materially dependent budget, cash-flow view, forecast vintage successor, goal trajectory, recurring-flow expectation, anomaly baseline, net-worth projection, review case, and report. Finance SHALL NOT require the user to repeat the same correction or manually maintain downstream copies.

OMN-FIN-002: Finance SHALL reuse Acquire/Ingest, Artifact, Data, Analyze, Model, Visualize, Evidence, History/Version, Triage/Clarify, Compose/View, Automate, Time, Space/Scope, Vault/Recovery, and the shared command/policy spine. Finance-specific semantics own financial meaning; shared capabilities own generic mechanics. No Excel workbook, analytical engine, dashboard cache, AI narrative, or imported statement format becomes a second writable financial authority.

Finance-owned canonical concepts SHOULD include only those that earn durable meaning, such as:

- FinancialAccount and explicit subject/owner identity;
- StatementSource / FinancialSource with source period and content identity;
- Transaction with lifecycle/status semantics;
- BalanceSnapshot;
- AssetPosition and LiabilityPosition;
- InvestmentHolding / Position and valuation observation;
- Debt terms/obligation state;
- BudgetPlan / BudgetLine where the user adopts a plan;
- FinancialGoal plus GoalAllocation/earmark where used;
- ForecastVintage and scenario/model assumptions;
- RecurringPattern / ExpectedCashFlow;
- RefundOrReimbursementReceivable;
- FinanceReviewCase;
- Merchant/Counterparty alias or classification rule;
- ParserProfile / transformation recipe;
- ReconciliationResult and unresolved difference.

Derived monthly summaries, dashboards, anomaly scores, forecasts, recommendations, and charts remain rebuildable projections unless explicitly adopted as canonical plan/decision records.

OMN-FIN-003: Finance acquisition SHALL recognize and separately model materially different source classes where present, including transaction accounts, credit cards, investments/brokerage, mortgages/loans/lines of credit, pay/income statements, tax/benefit documents, insurance/bills, receipts, and prior structured financial exports. A source class SHALL determine which facts can legitimately be extracted; balance movement alone SHALL NOT be reinterpreted as transaction detail, investment return, taxable income, or debt principal/interest without supporting evidence.

OMN-FIN-004: Finance SHALL use a parser cascade that prefers structured formats and direct extraction before heavier inference: standards/structured exports (for example OFX/QFX or equivalent admitted formats), CSV/XLSX/tabular exports, text-native PDF extraction, local OCR for scanned sources when justified, generic structural inference, then account/source-specific learned profiles. The selected parser/profile and material extraction confidence/provenance SHALL be retained. A known profile MAY auto-process later equivalent statements; structural drift SHALL reopen detection rather than silently misparse.

OMN-FIN-005: The exact original source Artifact SHALL remain preserved or referenceable according to retention policy, and every accepted normalized financial record SHALL retain enough lineage to trace `report/derived value -> calculation/rule -> canonical record -> extracted source row/region -> original source`. Raw extracted values and normalized values SHALL remain distinguishable where transformation changes representation.

OMN-FIN-006: Finance SHALL be exception-driven. On every import/update it SHOULD automatically identify source/account/period, deduplicate, normalize, reconcile, classify, match transfers/refunds/reversals, update derived state, run financial-integrity analysis, refresh budget/forecast/goals, and generate a concise change brief. It SHALL ask the user only about material unresolved exceptions, competing value judgments, or consequential actions that cannot be safely inferred, represented as unknown/provisional, or modeled as alternatives.

### Transaction and reconciliation semantics

OMN-FIN-007: Transaction normalization SHALL preserve, where available, source ID, source description, normalized merchant/counterparty, dates and posting semantics, exact amount, explicit currency, debit/credit direction, account, transaction class, transfer links, recurring state, categorization, source period, reconciliation state, review flags, and provenance. Currency amounts SHALL follow exact-money semantics under Section 4.3. Pending, authorized, posted, reversed, refunded, voided, corrected, and duplicate-import states SHALL not be silently collapsed when the distinction affects truth.

OMN-FIN-008: Reconciliation SHALL use every available invariant proportionate to the source, including opening balance + admitted activity = closing balance, statement summary totals, cross-account transfer pairing, credit-card payment pairing, refunds/reversals, investment cash movement, debt payment decomposition when supplied, and foreign-currency settlement evidence. Internal transfers and credit-card payments SHALL not be double-counted as income/spending. Unexplained differences SHALL remain explicit rather than being forced into an invented transaction or miscellaneous category.

OMN-FIN-009: Merchant/counterparty normalization and financial classification SHOULD learn from repeated evidence and confirmed corrections. Rules MAY use exact/prefix/pattern/source/account/amount/frequency context, but SHALL preserve original descriptors, confidence/provenance, scope, reversibility, and format-drift invalidation. A correction for one merchant/account/context SHALL NOT silently become a universal rule when materially different contexts exist.

### Financial analysis, budgets, liquidity, debt, investments, and net worth

OMN-FIN-010: Finance analysis SHOULD compute only supported metrics and clearly distinguish actuals, calculations, estimates, assumptions, forecasts, scenarios, and targets. Useful analyses include income, spending, essential/discretionary and fixed/variable composition, net cash flow, savings/savings rate, rolling and period comparisons, seasonality, recurring versus exceptional effects, fees/leakage, debt service/interest, liquidity, assets/liabilities, net worth, investment contributions/withdrawals/fees, and material category changes. When relevant to the supported decision or long-horizon plan, Finance SHALL also consider material downside, concentration, sequence-of-returns risk, inflation, taxes, fees, insurance/coverage/renewal exposure, fraud/scam risk, debt-service and cash-flow timing pressure, opportunity cost, and longevity/time-horizon assumptions rather than optimizing one headline return or balance. Short-term noise SHALL NOT be promoted to trend without sufficient evidence. Finance SHALL NOT compress multidimensional financial condition into one opaque universal health score; any summary indicator SHALL expose its underlying dimensions, assumptions, and material tradeoffs.

OMN-FIN-011: A useful Finance experience SHALL NOT require the user to hand-build a budget. When history supports it, Finance SHOULD generate an observed baseline from recurring obligations, historical distributions, seasonality, known future items, and structural changes. It SHALL keep at least `observed baseline`, `user-adopted target/plan`, `actual`, and `forecast` distinct. Repeated structural variance SHOULD trigger a stale-assumption warning or proposed plan revision rather than repeated moralized failure messaging.

OMN-FIN-012: Cash-flow/liquidity analysis SHALL distinguish current cash, available cash, restricted/earmarked cash, pending transactions, expected inflows/outflows, and borrowing capacity. Available credit, overdraft capacity, or undrawn debt SHALL NOT be counted as cash, savings, or goal funding. Forecasting SHOULD identify upcoming obligation clusters, expected minimum cash, reserve runway, timing gaps, and downside liquidity pressure before optimizing longer-horizon discretionary uses.

OMN-FIN-013: Debt analysis SHALL preserve balance, rate, fixed/variable nature, minimum/required payment, payment frequency, principal/interest/fees where supplied, maturity/amortization/renewal, and prepayment terms when available. For revolving credit/card obligations, preserve statement balance, payment due date, minimum due, materially relevant interest/grace-period semantics, and utilization/limit information only when actually supplied or reliably derived. Payoff/refinancing/consolidation or accelerated-payment comparisons SHALL be scenarios with explicit assumptions, liquidity effects, and costs; Finance SHALL not silently choose a value-laden debt strategy for the user.

OMN-FIN-014: Investment/portfolio analysis SHALL distinguish contributions/withdrawals, income/distributions, fees, realized transactions, market value, valuation date/source, cost/book/tax basis where actually supplied, realized versus unrealized change where supportable, and currency effects. Balance growth SHALL NOT be labeled investment performance when contributions or valuation uncertainty could explain it. When performance is computed, Finance SHALL use and name an appropriate method such as time-weighted or money-weighted return according to the question/data, preserve the period and cash-flow assumptions, and avoid comparing unlike methods as though they were equivalent. Current prices, tax treatment, product terms, and market-sensitive claims are runtime currentness data under OMN-FIN-026. Portfolio concentration, allocation, fee drag, currency exposure, and diversification observations MAY be derived when holdings/valuations are sufficiently complete, but SHALL not be presented as personalized investment advice or portfolio suitability merely because the data exists. When insurance records are supplied, Finance SHOULD preserve premiums, renewal/expiry, covered amount/limit, deductible, beneficiary/insured subject where applicable, and other financially relevant terms while treating adequacy, legal effect, exclusions, and replacement advice as evidence/qualified-professional questions rather than inferred certainty.

OMN-FIN-015: Net worth SHALL be a dated projection over supported asset and liability observations, not an invented universal score. Stale/estimated valuations SHALL be visibly identified, and a missing material account/asset/liability SHALL reduce or qualify completeness rather than being assumed zero.

### Financial goals and allocation planning

OMN-FIN-016: Financial goals SHALL support fixed-amount/date goals, dynamic/formula goals, and condition-based goals. Examples include emergency reserve, debt payoff, tax/annual-expense reserve, major purchase, travel, education, home/down payment, vehicle replacement, retirement/investment targets, and custom goals. A goal MAY derive its target from finance state (for example `N months of rolling essential expenses`) so the target self-updates when the underlying evidence changes.

A goal SHOULD retain, as applicable: target/condition, target date, priority supplied by the user, current funded amount, linked accounts/assets/liabilities, virtual allocations/earmarks, actual and planned contribution pace, required pace, forecast completion, assumptions, dependencies, competing goals, minimum acceptable outcome, confidence/data quality, and status.

OMN-FIN-017: Goal funding SHALL prevent double-counting the same money. When one account balance supports multiple goals, Finance SHALL use explicit logical allocations/earmarks or another auditable mechanism so apparent account balance is not simultaneously claimed as fully funding each goal. Unallocated value SHALL remain distinguishable. Goal allocation is planning state; it SHALL NOT imply a real bank subaccount or external transfer.

OMN-FIN-018: Goal trajectory SHALL compare progress against time and feasible cash-flow capacity, not only display percent complete. Where supported it SHOULD calculate required contribution pace, recent actual pace, forecast value/completion date, target-date slippage, and base/downside/upside cases. If requested goal contributions exceed sustainable forecast free cash flow, Finance SHALL expose the funding conflict and model alternatives (for example different dates, contributions, or discretionary assumptions) without silently deciding which legitimate goal should lose priority.

OMN-FIN-031: Finance SHALL treat financial goals as an interconnected goal portfolio over shared financial resources, obligations, accounts, assets, liabilities, income, and cash flows rather than independent progress bars. Savings, investments, debt payments, reserve allocations, expected income, and planned spending MAY contribute to, constrain, or compete among multiple goals through explicit relationships. A single source balance or cash-flow surplus SHALL be represented once and then allocated/referenced; it SHALL NOT be duplicated merely because retirement, FIRE, travel, home, education, emergency, tax, or other goals all depend on it.

OMN-FIN-032: Composite financial goals SHALL be expressible as dependency graphs rather than forced into one target amount. For example, a user-defined retirement or FIRE objective MAY depend on retirement date/age, spending assumptions, savings/investment balances, contribution rates, expected pensions/benefits, debt/housing obligations, taxes/fees, inflation/return assumptions, reserves, and other user-admitted factors. Finance SHALL expose which inputs are observed, current external facts, user assumptions, or modeled values; changing one input SHALL propagate through the composite goal. Omnevum SHALL NOT treat FIRE, retirement, home ownership, travel, or any other financial lifestyle choice as universally desirable or silently select assumptions that decide it for the user.

OMN-FIN-033: Finance SHALL interoperate with authorized non-financial plans when they have material financial consequences. Examples include a Travel plan contributing expected trip costs and dates to a vacation goal/cash-flow forecast; a Work/income change affecting savings, retirement, FIRE and debt trajectories; a Home/vehicle maintenance plan creating future reserves or obligations; or an education/household plan consuming a shared budget. The linked domain remains canonical owner of its own meaning; Finance receives only the financial projection needed for modeling, and finance-derived affordability does not become authority over the underlying life decision.

OMN-FIN-034: Finance SHOULD identify portfolio-level synergies and conflicts automatically. Examples include one savings increase advancing retirement and FIRE while delaying a near-term purchase; one debt payoff improving future free cash flow that can fund several goals; or a vacation plan competing with a home deposit for the same earmarked cash. The system SHOULD calculate shared effects once, propagate them across affected goals/scenarios, and surface material tradeoffs or Pareto-improving options without recommending a value judgment that the evidence cannot determine.

### Expected flows, leakage, refunds, and recurring patterns

OMN-FIN-019: Finance SHOULD infer recurring income, expenses, bills, subscriptions, annual/irregular obligations, and expected future flows using timing + counterparty + amount/context evidence. It SHOULD detect new or missing expected flows, delayed payroll/income, duplicate billing, price/frequency drift, annual renewals, and dormant recurrence returning. Calendar/holiday/pay-cycle variation and known one-offs SHALL be considered before escalation.

OMN-FIN-020: Refunds/reimbursements and other expected money-back items SHOULD be linked to their originating transaction/source and tracked by expected amount/date, received amount/date, status, and evidence. Finance SHOULD detect missing, partial, duplicate, late, or misdirected refunds/reimbursements where the evidence supports that conclusion.

OMN-FIN-021: Financial leakage analysis SHOULD identify and quantify material bank/account/card fees, late/overdraft/ATM/foreign-exchange fees, investment/trading/management fees, loan fees, interest/penalties, duplicate services, and recurring-cost drift. It SHALL call a cost avoidable only when the evidence supports avoidability; ordinary spending preference SHALL not be mislabeled as leakage merely because it is discretionary.

### Suspicious transaction and financial-integrity review

OMN-FIN-022: Finance SHALL run explainable financial-integrity/anomaly analysis over new and historical records. The objective includes possible unauthorized activity, scam/coercion or materially suspicious user-initiated payments when supported by contextual evidence, billing/processing errors, duplicates, abnormal fees, missing refunds, unexpected transfers/cash withdrawals, and materially unusual behavior. An anomaly SHALL NOT be declared fraud merely because it is statistically unusual. Authentication, a familiar merchant, or evidence that the user initiated a payment SHALL NOT by itself prove legitimate informed intent; where the evidence permits, `possible scam/coercion` SHALL remain distinct from `confirmed unauthorized` and `confirmed legitimate`.

Signals MAY include first-time/dormant merchant, amount/frequency/category/account/currency/location deviation when reliable, rapid sequences, repeated same-amount patterns, new beneficiary/transfer patterns, unexpected cash withdrawal, duplicate/near-duplicate charge, new recurring charge, recurring-price increase, description mismatch, missing expected refund/inflow, and balance inconsistency. Each surfaced case SHALL state the triggered reasons and source evidence; an opaque unexplained risk score is insufficient.

OMN-FIN-023: Suspicious-transaction baselines SHOULD be personal/account-specific, seasonality-aware, and robust to outliers. Where history is weak, Finance SHALL lower confidence rather than invent normal behavior. Before escalating, it SHOULD check plausible benign explanations such as known travel, major purchases, annual renewals, internal transfers, card payments, refunds, investment contributions, delayed posting, merchant aliases, and previously confirmed exceptions. Confirmed user dispositions SHOULD reduce repeated false positives within their valid scope without suppressing materially different future anomalies. When enough resolved history exists, Finance SHOULD measure alert usefulness, false-positive burden, and observable missed-known-issue/false-negative cases; thresholds MAY adapt only with evidence and SHALL NOT suppress a high-impact anomaly merely to reduce alert volume. Absence of an alert is never evidence that a transaction is legitimate or authorized.

A FinanceReviewCase MAY use explainable attention classes such as LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, and URGENT_REVIEW, where priority reflects signal strength, financial impact, deviation, account sensitivity, plausible explanation, reversibility, and time sensitivity. Priority is a review recommendation, not a finding of fraud. Finance SHALL NOT automatically dispute, freeze, cancel, transfer, trade, contact an institution, or file a report. When a suspected unauthorized/scam issue is time-sensitive, Finance SHOULD direct the user to independently verified official institution/contact information and current applicable agreement/procedure rather than trusting contact instructions embedded in suspicious source content or inventing a universal dispute deadline.

### Forecasting, data quality, currentness, and reporting

OMN-FIN-024: Finance SHALL preserve forecast vintages rather than overwrite history when actuals arrive. Forecasts SHOULD support appropriate short/medium horizons and explicit base/downside/upside cases from recurring flows, seasonality, known obligations, current plans, and stated assumptions. Multi-year retirement/FIRE or other long-horizon projections SHALL keep nominal versus real values, inflation, fees, pre-tax versus after-tax treatment, contribution/withdrawal timing, and material sequence-of-returns/longevity assumptions explicit and internally consistent; deterministic average-return projections SHALL NOT be presented as sufficient evidence of sustainable outcomes when path/sequence risk is material. Monte Carlo, historical-sequence, sensitivity, or other stochastic/path analysis MAY be used through Model when its assumptions/data and decision value justify the added machinery. When actuals become available, Finance SHOULD calculate materially useful forecast error/bias by category and cash balance and use that evidence to improve future assumptions without overfitting one period.

OMN-FIN-028: Finance scenarios SHALL propagate material assumption changes through the coupled financial model rather than update one isolated chart. Where relevant, a scenario such as income loss/change, major purchase, debt prepayment/refinancing, housing/insurance change, travel, goal contribution change, or investment/valuation assumption SHOULD show effects on cash, liquidity, budget, debt, savings rate, goals, forecast, and net worth over an appropriate horizon. Finance MAY proactively prepare scenarios when it detects a material future conflict, but SHALL present alternatives and assumptions rather than silently changing the user's plan.

OMN-FIN-025: Finance SHALL maintain explicit source/account coverage and data-quality state, including missing statements/periods, stale balances/valuations, unresolved reconciliation differences, uncertain merchants/categories, malformed/duplicated sources, unsupported estimates, and stale assumptions. A dependent conclusion SHALL be qualified or withheld when missing/stale data could materially change it. Source coverage SHOULD make missing periods inferable without requiring a manual checklist.

OMN-FIN-026: Volatile external financial facts such as tax law/rates, contribution limits, benefit rules, interest rates, product terms/fees, regulatory requirements, market prices, exchange rates, or institution-specific dispute/reporting deadlines SHALL be verified from current authoritative sources when material. Jurisdiction/effective date/status SHALL be explicit. Finance provides analysis/planning/navigation, not unsupported legal, tax, accounting, investment, or regulatory authority; qualified professional or official confirmation remains an external gate where required.

OMN-FIN-027: Imported financial files are untrusted. Spreadsheet formulas/macros/active content, PDF scripts/actions/links, embedded remote resources, and extracted markup SHALL remain inert unless a separately qualified workflow explicitly requires otherwise. Formula injection and spreadsheet round-trip risks SHALL be tested where spreadsheet import/export is claimed. Corrections, source withdrawal/replacement, or changed parser rules SHALL invalidate and recompute materially dependent derived views, forecasts, goals, anomaly baselines, and reports rather than leaving stale conclusions active.

### Finance presentation and autonomous update brief

The Resources/Finance experience MAY use dashboards, analytical tables, and drill-down views even though global Home remains a bounded consideration surface. Finance presentation SHOULD answer, with traceable evidence: `Where am I?`, `What changed?`, `What is coming?`, `What is unusual or wrong?`, and `Am I progressing toward my goals?`

After a material import/update, Finance SHOULD automatically produce a concise evidence-linked brief covering only applicable items: current position, material changes, trends, budget variance, cash-flow/liquidity outlook, goals, debt/investment position, suspicious/review items, recurring/fee/refund/leakage changes, forecast, data-quality limitations, and the smallest set of high-value next actions. A quiet update with no material exception is a valid outcome.

Finance SHALL support bounded portable export through existing Share/Artifact capabilities, including human-readable reports and tabular open/interchange formats such as CSV and qualified spreadsheet export where useful. Export is a projection/portable copy; it SHALL NOT make the spreadsheet/file the canonical Finance database.

# 3. Human experience, personalization, and internationalization

## 3.1 Primary surfaces

The default system surfaces are:

- Home
- Capture
- Search / Explore
- Assistant
- Review
- System / Recovery

### Home

Home answers: **What deserves consideration?**

It SHALL NOT become a dashboard wall, engagement feed, notification center, or duplicated task/calendar surface.

Home MAY surface a small bounded set of traceable items such as:

- stale assumptions;
- meaningful change;
- unresolved risk;
- conflicting evidence;
- cross-domain conflict;
- credible synergy opportunity;
- broken dependency;
- unvalidated intervention;

Capture routing MAY be suggested by deterministic rules or AI but SHALL preserve a non-AI manual path and canonical owner semantics. Inputs with a clear accepted owner/type MAY be committed directly through the owning semantic command; ambiguous, incomplete, mixed, or deliberately inboxed input SHOULD be preservable for Triage/Clarify without forcing premature classification.

OMN-UX-006: Where the target/browser supports a qualified Web Share Target or successor mechanism, Omnevum SHOULD accept user-shared text, URLs, and supported files directly into Capture/Acquire as staged untrusted input. Unsupported targets SHALL retain ordinary in-app paste, URL entry, file picker, drag/drop where appropriate, clipboard, and import paths; share-sheet support is progressive enhancement rather than a core correctness dependency.

### Assistant

Assistant is a user-facing interface to the shared AI capability. It does not own AI, credentials, permissions, or canonical data.

### Review

Review SHOULD support daily, weekly, monthly, project, decision, intervention, domain, and whole-system review templates without imposing engagement pressure.

### System / Recovery

System owns settings, privacy, storage, sync, backup, connectors, AI routes, personalization, localization, diagnostics, updates, health, Safe Mode, and Recovery.

## 3.2 Mobile-first responsive interaction

OMN-UX-001: Omnevum SHALL be designed mobile-first for touch and one-handed use where practical, then progressively enhance for tablets and desktops.

OMN-UX-002: The active content surface SHALL receive most usable space on compact targets. Omnevum SHALL NOT force a miniature desktop/window metaphor onto phones.

OMN-UX-003: Ordinary first-party content SHOULD reflow at approximately 320 CSS pixels equivalent width, subject to legitimate intrinsically two-dimensional exceptions.

OMN-UX-004: Critical workflows SHALL NOT depend solely on hover, drag, right-click, double-click, mouse wheel, multi-touch, precision pointer, or undiscoverable gestures.

OMN-UX-005: Required controls/state SHALL survive portrait/landscape, safe areas/notches, virtual keyboard, text zoom/scaling, split screen, live resize, foldable/dynamic viewport, and browser/installed transitions on claimed targets.

OMN-UX-007: Every interactive target that commits, dismisses, snoozes, navigates, or otherwise carries consequence SHALL present a hit area of at least 44 x 44 CSS pixels, independent of its painted glyph size. Adjacent independent targets SHALL retain at least 8 CSS pixels of non-interactive separation. Density preferences MAY reduce painted size but SHALL NOT reduce the 44 x 44 hit bound.

OMN-UX-008: Primary routine actions - Capture invocation, lens switching, staging-item disposition, and confirm/cancel on any bottom sheet - SHALL be reachable within the lower 50% of the compact viewport. Destructive or irreversible controls SHALL NOT be placed in the dominant thumb-rest zone adjacent to a routine control of similar affordance.

OMN-UX-009: The Home, Capture, lens, Assistant, and System/Recovery surfaces SHALL reflow at 320 CSS pixels equivalent width to a single column with no horizontal scrolling of first-party chrome or content, at 200% text scaling, with no clipped, overlapped, or unreachable control. Intrinsically two-dimensional content (maps, spatial canvases, wide analytical tables) MAY scroll within an explicitly bounded region that does not propagate horizontal scroll to the page.

## 3.4 Personal ownership and branding

Omnevum is the upstream distribution; the running personal system belongs to the user.

OMN-PERS-002: The user SHALL be able to rename and rebrand the running system at any time without migrating canonical records.

Example supported state: distribution = Omnevum; personal system name = JohnOS.

OMN-PERS-003: User presentation identity MAY include system name, short name, tagline, logo, icon, avatar, background, splash presentation, Home heading, terminology, and module/lens labels.

OMN-PERS-004: Upstream Omnevum provenance, exact version, license/notices, and security/release identity SHALL remain inspectable even when the visible presentation is fully personalized.

OMN-PERS-005: User branding SHALL survive Omnevum updates unless explicitly reset.

OMN-PERS-006: Personalization SHALL NOT create security principals, permissions, canonical ownership, record identity, or cryptographic authority.

OMN-PERS-007: Subject to accessibility, security, Recovery, and semantic-integrity guardrails, users SHALL have maximum practical freedom to make the running system their own, including navigation ordering/visibility, Home composition, pinned surfaces, layout/density, typography, iconography, backgrounds, optional sounds/haptics, motion, default views, lens/module display names, custom views, and presentation profiles. A cosmetic or organizational preference SHALL NOT require a source-code fork.

OMN-PERS-008: Personalization overlays SHALL be independently reversible, resettable, exportable, and versioned separately from canonical life-domain data. A broken overlay SHALL be bypassable without deleting it or canonical records.

OMN-PERS-009: User-importable branding/theme/language/presentation packs SHALL be declarative and inert by default. They SHALL NOT gain script execution, network, credential, permission, or canonical-mutation authority merely by being installed as presentation content.

## 3.7 Localization and multilingual architecture

Stable semantic identity SHALL be independent of English labels.

Omnevum SHALL distinguish:

- system UI language;
- domain terminology translation;
- user terminology overrides;
- user-content language;
- locale/region;
- time zone;
- number/date formatting;
- currency;
- measurement units;
- text direction.

Canonical numbers, money, measurements, dates/times, and enums SHALL remain structured semantic values rather than localized strings.

Changing language SHALL NOT migrate canonical records or silently translate user-authored content.

User terminology overlays MAY override localized defaults while preserving stable semantic IDs.

OMN-I18N-001: The localization system SHALL support Unicode, locale-aware formatting, pluralization, interpolation, fallback, and directionality.

OMN-I18N-002: Right-to-left presentation SHALL be architecturally supportable from the design-system level rather than retrofitted through component-specific hacks.

OMN-I18N-003: Critical Help/Recovery content SHALL be available offline for every language claimed as fully supported.

OMN-I18N-004: Language support states SHALL be explicit, for example COMPLETE, PARTIAL, EXPERIMENTAL, OUTDATED, UNSUPPORTED.

OMN-I18N-005: Pseudolocalization, long-string expansion, Unicode edge cases, and RTL checks SHALL be part of the UI test strategy.

OMN-I18N-006: Language packs SHALL be versioned declarative presentation data, not executable authority. Missing, stale, or failed optional language resources SHALL fall back safely to a qualified built-in language without blocking Recovery.

OMN-I18N-007: User-authored/imported content language SHALL remain independent of UI language. Translation, transliteration, summarization, and localization of user content are DERIVED artifacts unless the user explicitly creates or adopts a new canonical artifact; the original source/provenance SHALL remain available.

OMN-I18N-008: Search/indexing SHALL support mixed-language Unicode content through locale-appropriate or language-agnostic derived indexing as qualified. Search indexes, tokenization products, transliterations, and embeddings remain rebuildable projections and SHALL NOT alter canonical text.

OMN-I18N-009: AI/tool authority SHALL use stable semantic IDs and typed operations, not localized display labels, brand names, or user terminology as security/command authority. The Assistant MAY speak in the user's preferred language and terminology while its tool calls remain language-neutral.

OMN-I18N-010: Browser/OS-controlled installed-PWA localization (including host-captured app name/icon metadata) SHALL be capability-tested and reported honestly. ## 3.8 Normative visual system

OMN-VIS-001: Omnevum has one normative visual/interaction system with three built-in visual families: Alpha, Beta, and Gamma as defined in Section 3.6. The families may differ in typography, spacing rhythm, separators, grouping, and metadata treatment, but they SHALL render the same semantic state, evidence, uncertainty, commands, navigation reachability, telemetry truth, accessibility obligations, and Recovery authority. Concept Gamma remains the distribution default; selecting Alpha or Beta is an ordinary presentation choice, not a forked product surface.

### 3.8.1 Typography

OMN-VIS-002: Every built-in typography stack SHALL resolve offline without a network font fetch, honor user text scaling, preserve a minimum rendered text size of 11px at the 16px reference root before user scaling, and select locale-appropriate fallbacks when the preferred stack poorly serves a script. Typography MAY change hierarchy and density but SHALL NOT change semantic prominence, hide state, or compress a required control below its minimum hit target.

OMN-VIS-003: The built-in family typography baselines are:

- Metadata / evidence: `ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace`; 11px equivalent; weight 500; line height 1.4.

**Concept Beta - Humanist Editorial / Contextual Flow**

- Surface title: `ui-serif, "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Georgia, serif`; 22px equivalent; weight 600; line height 1.25.

- Body / record: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`; 15px equivalent; weight 400; line height 1.55.
- Metadata / badge: same system sans stack; 12px equivalent; weight 500; line height 1.4.
- Editorial hierarchy is carried by whitespace, paragraph rhythm, and grouping rather than ornamental decoration.

**Concept Gamma - Industrial Utility / Segmented HUD**

- Stack: `ui-monospace, "JetBrains Mono", "SF Mono", "Roboto Mono", "DejaVu Sans Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace`.

- Section heading: 16px equivalent; weight 500; line height 1.35; tracking 0.
- Body / record: 13px equivalent; weight 400; line height 1.55; tracking 0.
- Metadata / badge: 11px equivalent; weight 400; line height 1.4; tracking +0.02em.
- Only weights 400 and 500 are used by default within the Gamma stack.

All stated sizes SHALL be implemented with relative units or an equivalent mechanism that honors user scaling; the pixel values above state the reference values at a 16px root with no additional scaling.

OMN-VIS-004: Serif, sans-serif, or monospace presentation SHALL NOT be forced onto user-authored content or a language/script that the selected stack serves poorly. The Presentation capability SHALL select a qualified locale-appropriate face and preserve correct directionality and legibility. Theme identity is subordinate to localization/accessibility correctness.

### 3.8.2 Surface layering

OMN-VIS-005: Exactly three semantic painted planes are admitted by the built-in families: canvas, surface, and raised surface. Alpha emphasizes borders and ruled regions; Beta emphasizes spacing, card grouping, and soft explicit separators; Gamma emphasizes precision grid alignment and machine-state grouping. Built-in depth SHALL be conveyed by plane value, border/separator, grouping, and spacing rather than drop shadow, blur, glow, or gradient. At most one floating layer (bottom sheet, dialog, or menu) SHALL be presented at a time; a second required floating layer SHALL replace rather than stack on the first.

OMN-VIS-006: Bounded progress, confidence, and completeness SHALL always carry an adjacent explicit textual value or state and SHALL never be the sole carrier of a consequential condition. Gamma SHOULD use segmented indicators by default. Alpha MAY use ruled/typographic segments or fractions. Beta MAY use quiet step markers or textual progress. A theme family SHALL NOT alter the underlying value, threshold, or command behavior.

### 3.8.3 System telemetry presentation

OMN-VIS-007: Every primary surface SHALL present persistent, honest, non-decorative telemetry covering at minimum: storage locality (local-only versus replicated), last successful backup/sync outcome and staleness, pending Effect/Outbox depth, and degraded-capability state. Telemetry SHALL report UNKNOWN rather than imply success, SHALL be derived from real state rather than from an optimistic UI assumption, and SHALL NOT be rendered as an engagement metric, score, streak, or gamified counter. Alpha MAY render it as terse ruled metadata, Beta as a quiet contextual line, and Gamma as HUD-like machine telemetry; the information and activation path remain equivalent.

### 3.8.4 Motion, haptics, and feedback

OMN-VIS-009: Motion is admitted only to show the origin of a state change - where a sheet came from, where a dismissed item went, which record a result belongs to. Decorative, looping, attention-seeking, celebratory, and progress-theatre motion SHALL NOT be used. Admitted transitions SHALL complete within 200ms and SHALL NOT delay the availability of the resulting control.

OMN-VIS-010: A haptic tick MAY accompany a committed semantic mutation, an accepted Triage disposition, and an undo. Haptics SHALL NOT be used for arrival of information, for suggestions, or for any event the user did not initiate. Haptics SHALL be independently disableable and SHALL never be the sole carrier of an outcome.

OMN-VIS-011: A zero-motion mode SHALL be available as an accessibility setting and SHALL be adopted automatically when the platform reports a reduced-motion preference. In zero-motion mode every admitted transition becomes an instantaneous state swap with no loss of function, no loss of information, and no substituted fade.

### 3.8.5 Built-in family identity and parity

OMN-VIS-046: Concept Alpha SHALL preserve a monochrome/near-monochrome foundation, structured borders, compact information rhythm, typography-first hierarchy, and zero decorative elevation. It SHALL NOT reduce accessibility, evidence visibility, touch geometry, or content legibility in order to appear denser.

OMN-VIS-047: Concept Beta SHALL preserve serene spacing, warm-neutral palette treatment, distinct contextual card grouping, calm empty states, and soft but explicit separators. It SHALL NOT turn Home into a lifestyle feed, remove machine-state honesty, or hide evidence/uncertainty in order to appear calmer.

OMN-VIS-048: Concept Gamma SHALL preserve monospace metadata where locale-appropriate, precision alignment, segmented system indicators, and explicit machine/locality telemetry. It SHALL NOT force monospace onto unsupported scripts/user content or turn operational telemetry into a decorative score.

OMN-VIS-049: Switching among Alpha, Beta, and Gamma SHALL be a pure presentation substitution over the same surface anatomy and semantic command paths. For a fixed application state and density preference, family switching SHALL preserve the same Home consideration membership/order, Capture staging items and provenance, lens reachability, record identities/relationships, Assistant scope, Recovery access, admitted commands, and consequential-state meaning.

## 3.9 Layout anatomy of primary surfaces

OMN-VIS-008: The anatomy below is normative for the compact (mobile) default of each surface. It constrains arrangement and required elements, not visual taste, and SHALL hold under OMN-UX-007 through OMN-UX-009.

OMN-VIS-012: Where this section admits a quiet default and an explicit alternative, the explicit alternative SHALL be reachable from the surface it concerns without navigating to a settings tree, SHALL be a persisted user preference rather than a per-session state that silently resets, and SHALL NOT be presented as a corrective for a deficient default.

### Home

- Header: a thin persistent telemetry strip per OMN-VIS-007 and Section 3.10.
- Consideration list: a bounded set of traceable items, default maximum five, ordered by consequence rather than recency, presented as a bounded stack whose spacing follows the active theme family and density preference.
- Each item SHALL render, within the item itself and without navigation: source/evidence count with a path to the sources; an uncertainty marker with an explicit value or an explicit UNKNOWN; a "why this appeared" statement naming the originating signals; and one-tap snooze and dismiss controls meeting OMN-UX-007.
- OMN-VIS-013: Home SHALL offer a focus mode presenting one consideration item at a time at larger type with expanded targets. Focus mode SHALL change presentation only: the same items, the same order, the same evidence, the same admitted commands. It SHALL NOT hide items, alter ranking, gate advancement on disposition, or impose a sequence the user must complete.
- Terminal row: explicit empty/queue state. "Nothing deserves attention" SHALL be rendered as a first-class calm state, never as an error, prompt, or opportunity to engage.
- Home SHALL NOT render KPI walls, rings, streaks, engagement counters, infinite feeds, or a duplicated task/calendar list.

### Capture

- Half-height bottom sheet over an inert dimmed backdrop. The backdrop SHALL be non-interactive except for an explicit dismiss affordance; dismissal SHALL NOT discard staged input without confirmation.
- Modality row: text, audio/voice, scan/camera, and URL/file, each an independent target meeting OMN-UX-007, each degrading explicitly and individually when the underlying Device/Input capability is unavailable.
- Staging queue: each entry SHALL display raw input provenance (source, capture time, modality) before any interpretation, and routing suggestions SHALL be rendered as proposals with their origin (deterministic rule or AI) and confidence, visually subordinate to the raw content.
- Commit, defer-to-Triage, and discard SHALL be distinct admitted commands. No suggestion SHALL self-commit on timeout, scroll, or sheet dismissal.

### Triage / inbox

- OMN-VIS-014: The inbox SHALL present staged items as a scannable list carrying, per row, raw content identity, modality, capture time, and the proposed owner with its confidence or an explicit UNKNOWN. Multi-select SHALL be available for accepting, deferring, or discarding several items through the same admitted commands used for one.
- OMN-VIS-015: Any row SHALL expand in place to the full single-item view showing complete provenance, the full proposal with its origin, and per-item split, link, route, defer, reference, and delete commands. Expansion SHALL NOT be required to act, and batch action SHALL NOT be available for any disposition whose consequences the collapsed row cannot honestly state.
- A batch action SHALL report its per-item outcome individually. A partial failure SHALL NOT be reported as success and SHALL NOT silently drop the failed items from staging.

### Record detail

- Continuous scroll: identity and lens breadcrumb, then fields, then relationships, then history, then evidence.
- OMN-VIS-016: A sticky segment bar SHALL offer direct jumps to fields, links, history, and evidence. The bar navigates within one scrollable document; it SHALL NOT hide sections behind tabs, SHALL NOT defer their loading in a way that makes a section unreachable when a derived index is degraded, and SHALL indicate which segment is currently in view.
- Relationships SHALL render as navigable references naming the owning lens path, never as embedded editable copies.
- Every field SHALL be editable only through the owning semantic command; the detail surface holds no independent mutation authority.

### Search / Explore

- Default state: a single query field, results grouped by owning lens, each result stating why it matched.
- OMN-VIS-017: Facet chips (lens, time, type, Space/Scope, has-artifact and equivalents) SHALL be available on demand from the query field without being present by default. An applied chip SHALL be visible, individually removable, and expressible in the query itself so that a filtered search is inspectable and reproducible rather than hidden UI state.
- A filtered result set MAY be saved as a named view. A saved view is a declarative Compose/View definition per Section 5.20; it creates no writable copy and no separate index authority.
- Results SHALL honor the active Space/Scope bound and SHALL state that bound on the surface.

### Assistant

- OMN-VIS-018: The Assistant is reachable both as a standalone conversation surface and as a contextual panel invoked from any record or lens. Both entries SHALL be the same capability under one scope model: the context bound is always explicit, always narrowable, and never silently widened by the entry point used. Invoking from a record SHALL default the bound to that record and its authorized relationships, and SHALL state that bound.
- Every surface SHALL declare the active provider/route and disclosure class at all times.
- Every proposed action SHALL be rendered as a proposal carrying the semantic command it would invoke and the records it would touch, and SHALL require an explicit admitted confirmation.
- The surface SHALL remain present and honestly degraded when all AI routes are disabled; it SHALL NOT become the only path to any core function.

### Review

- OMN-VIS-019: Review is a passive shelf of available templates. It SHALL NOT schedule itself, impose due dates, count streaks, or notify. A template that is never opened SHALL produce no pressure of any kind.
- A selected template runs as a guided stepper with segmented progress, skippable at every step, abandonable without loss, and resumable without penalty. Partial completion is a valid terminal state.
- Prompts within a stepper SHALL cite the records that motivate them and SHALL offer the admitted command to act, rather than asking the user to remember and navigate.

### Lenses

- Persistent bottom lens navigation. OMN-VIS-020: The bar SHALL present four user-pinned lenses plus an overflow control opening an eight-item grid sheet covering all default lenses of Section 2.2: Direction, People, Self, Resources, Work, Environment, Knowledge, Change.
- The overflow grid SHALL present every lens at equal prominence with full accessible names, SHALL be reachable in one interaction from any primary surface, and SHALL NOT rank, hide, or demote a lens by usage.
- The currently active lens SHALL remain visible in the bar even when it is not pinned; pinning state SHALL NOT make any lens unreachable.
- Each lens target SHALL meet OMN-UX-007 and the bar SHALL remain a single reachable row at 320 CSS pixels.
- The active lens view SHALL present structured canonical records, their relationships, and a context breadcrumb naming the owning lens path.
- A lens is a projection. The lens surface SHALL NOT create writable state outside the owning semantic command path, SHALL NOT duplicate a canonical record it does not own, and SHALL render cross-lens relationships as navigable references rather than embedded copies.

### System / Recovery

- Recovery entry SHALL be reachable within two interactions from any primary surface and SHALL NOT depend on the user's presentation profile, custom theme, language pack, or any optional module loading successfully.
- Recovery surfaces SHALL render from the bound baseline tokens of Section 3.6.1 under Safe Presentation Mode, at minimum 13px body text, with no reliance on gesture, hover, or color-only state.
- Destructive operations SHALL be separated in position and affordance from routine operations and SHALL state their exact scope and reversibility before confirmation.

## 3.10 State honesty, reversibility, and confirmation

### 3.10.1 Telemetry escalation

OMN-VIS-021: The telemetry required by OMN-VIS-007 SHALL be presented in its quiet healthy form as a thin persistent strip on every primary surface, occupying one line, and SHALL open the System detail on activation. OMN-VIS-007 governs its content; this requirement governs only its resting presentation.

OMN-VIS-022: When a telemetry condition crosses a defined consequence threshold - backup staleness beyond policy, an Effect/Outbox item failing delivery, a capability degraded, a conflict unresolved beyond policy, or storage pressure - the condition SHALL additionally escalate into a first-class Home consideration item carrying the same evidence, "why this appeared", and disposition controls as any other item. Escalation thresholds SHALL be inspectable and adjustable by the user. The strip SHALL NOT become the sole location of a consequential warning, and a healthy system SHALL NOT manufacture escalations to appear active.

### 3.10.2 Reversibility and confirmation

OMN-VIS-023: Confirmation depth SHALL be proportional to reversibility, not to the surface or the verb used.

- A reversible operation SHALL execute immediately and present an undo affordance with a segmented remaining-time indicator and an explicit numeric value. Expiry of the undo window SHALL move the record to a recoverable state for the owning domain's retention policy rather than destroying it.
- A hard or externally-observable operation - destruction beyond the retention window, key or credential destruction, wipe, irreversible external effect delivery, or an operation the owning domain declares non-reversible - SHALL require pre-confirmation stating its exact blast radius: the records affected, the relationships that will be left orphaned or preserved, the artifacts involved, and the reversibility window or its absence.
- OMN-VIS-024: A pre-confirmation SHALL state real computed consequences derived from the actual graph, never a generic warning template. Where the consequence cannot be computed, the surface SHALL say so explicitly rather than presenting an optimistic or absent blast radius.
- OMN-VIS-043: An operation that enqueues an externally-observable effect SHALL hold that effect undelivered for at least the duration of its undo window, and undo SHALL withdraw it from the Outbox. Where the effect cannot be held - an immediate third-party call, a delivery the transport will not defer - the operation is not reversible and SHALL take the pre-confirmation path instead. An undo affordance SHALL NOT be shown over an effect that has already left the system.
- Undo SHALL NOT be offered for an operation that cannot actually be undone.

### 3.10.3 Uncertainty presentation

OMN-VIS-025: Confidence, provenance depth, and staleness are recorded on every derived value but presented quietly by default: a field SHALL NOT display a confidence indicator merely because a value exists.

OMN-VIS-026: A per-surface "show confidence" toggle SHALL reveal segmented confidence, source count, and last-verified time for every field in view. The toggle SHALL be a persisted preference and SHALL change presentation only.

OMN-VIS-027: A genuine conflict - two admitted sources disagreeing on the same canonical value - SHALL surface unconditionally regardless of the toggle, SHALL retain both values with their provenance rather than silently selecting one, SHALL remain in an explicit unresolved state until resolved through an admitted command, and SHALL NOT be resolved by recency, confidence score, or any automatic rule without the user's admitted choice.

## 3.11 Personalization and accessibility surfaces

OMN-VIS-028: Presentation preferences SHALL be editable in place on the surface they affect - lens pinning from the lens bar, density and text scale from the surface being read, terminology from the label being renamed - and the central System > Presentation list SHALL mirror every such preference, reflecting in-place edits immediately and writing back identically. Neither path SHALL expose a preference the other cannot reach. Both SHALL write one presentation profile per Section 3.4, independently reversible and resettable.

OMN-VIS-029: Accessibility SHALL be offered as named profiles - at minimum standard, low vision, motor/large target, and low cognitive load - each of which sets a known-good combination of the underlying independent settings. Selecting a profile SHALL expand to reveal and permit editing of every individual setting it changed, and a user edit SHALL NOT be silently reverted by the profile. Profiles are presentation state only.

OMN-VIS-030: Accessibility profiles SHALL NOT gate any function, SHALL NOT be presented as remedial, and SHALL NOT reduce the accessible baseline of the standard profile. A platform-reported accessibility preference SHALL be honored on first run without requiring the user to find a setting.

## 3.12 Lifecycle surfaces

### 3.12.1 First run

OMN-VIS-031: First run SHALL be a cold open: usable immediately, with no account, no configuration, no tour, and no mandatory step. The opening state SHALL state honestly that the system is local-only and empty, and SHALL offer capture as the single obvious action.

OMN-VIS-032: A shaping prompt - selecting pinned lenses, terminology, density, and presentation - SHALL be offered later, triggered by accumulated real use rather than by a timer or launch count, SHALL be dismissible permanently, and SHALL remain available on demand from System thereafter. It SHALL NOT be a precondition for any capability, and every lens SHALL remain available whether or not it is pinned.

### 3.12.2 Update and migration

OMN-VIS-033: A shell, asset, or presentation update that does not migrate canonical data SHALL apply silently at the next safe opportunity and SHALL be recorded in an inspectable version ledger with the applied version, time, and a rollback path where one exists.

OMN-VIS-044: A shell update and a canonical schema migration are separately gated. Where the runtime activates a new application shell independently of user action - including service-worker activation under Section 6.5 - the new shell SHALL detect a pending unapproved schema migration, SHALL start in a read-compatible state against the unmigrated data or refuse to take control, and SHALL NOT perform, begin, or partially apply that migration on activation. The migration prompt is presented in the running session; declining leaves the system fully usable on the prior schema. Exactly one shell version SHALL be in control at a time, and a shell that cannot operate against the unmigrated data SHALL report that condition rather than migrating to resolve it.

OMN-VIS-034: An update carrying a canonical schema migration SHALL NOT apply silently. It SHALL prompt before applying, stating the migrations involved, the record classes affected, the verified state and age of the current backup, and the rollback path or its absence. Where no verified recent backup exists, the surface SHALL say so and offer to create one before proceeding. An interrupted migration SHALL follow Section 8.4 crash-consistency and SHALL NOT silently reset data.

### 3.12.3 Share and export scope

OMN-VIS-035: A bounded share or export SHALL be constructed through an explicit scope builder in which each linked record, artifact, relationship, and history element is individually included or excluded, defaulting to excluded, with the transitive consequence of each inclusion stated.

OMN-VIS-036: The final confirmation step SHALL be a recipient-eye preview rendering exactly the content that will leave the system, including every artifact and field, and SHALL NOT be skippable. The surface SHALL state explicitly what is not included - credentials, AI authority, out-of-scope linked records, and unrelated provenance - and the exported payload SHALL match the preview byte-for-byte in content.

### 3.12.4 Recovery and Safe Presentation Mode

OMN-VIS-037: The ordinary Recovery path SHALL be a degraded rendering of the normal interface using the Safe Presentation baseline of Section 3.6.1: the same command vocabulary and primary layout grammar, fewer dependencies, and a known-good Gamma visual family even when the user's active family or custom profile is broken. It SHALL state what failed, what remains intact, and the verified state of the Vault, and SHALL offer repair, baseline-theme, and restore paths.

OMN-VIS-038: A last-resort Recovery console SHALL exist for the case where the ordinary degraded path itself cannot render. It SHALL be visually distinct from the normal interface so that its exceptional status is unmistakable, SHALL depend on no theme, language pack, font, module, index, or network, SHALL be reachable through a documented stable route that survives a broken application shell, and SHALL at minimum report canonical record integrity and Vault state and permit an immediate export.

OMN-VIS-039: Neither Recovery path SHALL delete a user's presentation profile, language pack, or canonical data as a precondition of recovering.

### 3.12.5 Data exit and system retirement

OMN-VIS-040: System SHALL present a permanent, always-available export surface offering at minimum: a full portable Vault archive, a human-readable export in durable open formats, and an artifact-originals export. Each SHALL state its record count and size, and SHALL be usable without Omnevum present. The export surface SHALL NOT be placed behind a retention prompt, a reason-for-leaving question, or any friction.

OMN-VIS-041: An optional guided retirement flow SHALL be available, sequencing export, verification of that export, wipe of origin storage, and an optional successor/handoff note recording where the data now lives and what is needed to read it.

OMN-VIS-045: Before the wipe step, the retirement flow SHALL enumerate every known copy of the user's data: origin storage, each configured replica/sync endpoint, off-origin backups, and any remote-backed artifact tier, each with its last-known state and location. For each, the flow SHALL state plainly whether Omnevum can delete it, can only request its deletion, or cannot reach it at all, and SHALL NOT report retirement as complete while a copy Omnevum cannot verify as deleted remains enumerated. Losing reach to an endpoint SHALL be reported as UNKNOWN rather than omitted from the enumeration.

OMN-VIS-042: The wipe step SHALL be blocked until an export has been verified within the same flow, or until the user explicitly and separately records that they intend to destroy the data without retaining a copy. Verification SHALL mean the export was read back and checked, not that a download was initiated. Retirement SHALL never be initiated by Omnevum, by an update, by storage pressure, or by any automatic condition.


# 4. Semantic and canonical data architecture

## 4.1 Minimal semantic kernel

The shared kernel SHOULD remain small and stable. Initial generic concepts:

- Entity
- Relationship
- Need
- Outcome
- Constraint
- Assumption
- Observation
- Evidence
- Measure
- Decision
- Risk
- Intervention
- Review
- Source
- ExternalReference

Specialist modules MAY extend these concepts but SHALL NOT redefine shared semantics incompatibly.

## 4.2 Canonical ownership

OMN-DATA-001: Every mutable semantic fact SHALL have one canonical owner.

Other views/modules MAY reference, query, project, visualize, analyze, or propose changes but SHALL NOT create unsynchronized writable copies.

OMN-DATA-002: Cross-domain synthesis SHALL be a projection over owned records and typed relationships, not a second universal writable harmony database.

Each owner/domain SHALL declare:

- purpose;
- source/provenance;
- storage;
- sensitivity/disclosure;
- retention where applicable;
- correction;
- export;
- deletion;
- backup;
- migration;
- Recovery.

## 4.3 Canonical record envelope

Where applicable, canonical records SHALL carry:

- stable opaque ID;
- record type;
- owning domain/module;
- schema version;
- created/modified/effective/observation time semantics;
- provenance/source;
- truth/evidence class;
- sensitivity/privacy metadata;
- revision/conflict state;
- soft-delete/tombstone state where needed.

Globally scoped IDs SHALL be collision-resistant and independent of mutable labels/branding.

Money SHALL use exact decimal or integer-minor-unit semantics with explicit currency. Measurements SHALL store units separately from localized display.

OMN-DATA-006: Records about a person, dependant, household member, pet, organization, asset, account, place, or other subject SHALL preserve explicit subject/entity identity where confusion is materially possible. Shared record types or identical measures SHALL NOT merge different subjects merely because their schemas match. Subject relationships and authority/consent remain distinct from record ownership.

## 4.4 Truth and provenance

At minimum distinguish:

- authoritative imported/official record;
- user observation;
- source claim;
- assumption;
- estimate;
- derived value;
- AI-generated hypothesis;
- simulated/model output;
- unknown/stale/disputed state.

Acceptance of an AI suggestion may authorize storage/action but SHALL NOT silently convert a derived claim into observed truth.

## 4.5 Derived state

Search indexes, embeddings, thumbnails, summaries, caches, dashboards, recommendations, AI synthesis, and generated projections are subordinate derived state unless an explicit user action creates a new canonical artifact.

Derived state SHALL be rebuildable where technically possible, inherit source sensitivity/authorization, retain sufficient provenance, and be invalidated/rebuilt when controlling source meaning or access changes.

## 4.6 Relationships

Relationships SHALL be typed and SHALL NOT transfer canonical ownership merely by linking records.

Omnevum SHOULD support cross-domain questions such as:

- show everything materially affecting this goal;
- what changed after this intervention;
- which obligations compete for the same time or money;
- which decisions rely on this assumption;
- which records depend on this source;
- which one action or resource change could advance several goals;
- which goals are drawing on the same constrained resource;
- what downstream plans or forecasts would change if this record changed.

# 5. Shared capability fabric

A capability is promoted to the shared platform only when a stable cross-domain boundary or multiple real consumers justify it. DRY SHALL NOT erase domain-specific ownership, privacy, validation, authority, or lifecycle rules.

OMN-CAP-001: Shared capabilities SHALL expose semantic contracts independent of any one lens/module/upstream implementation, and SHALL remain replaceable behind those contracts where a material external implementation is used.

OMN-CAP-002: A capability discovered repeatedly across owner projects or qualified FOSS SHALL trigger explicit generalization analysis before a second or third bespoke implementation is accepted.

Initial capability catalogue:

## 5.1 Acquire / Ingest

Opt-in personal-data acquisition from user-selected files, exports, archives, URLs, browser-supported connectors, device/provider exports, and future admitted sources.

Acquire/Ingest SHALL support, where applicable:

- source recognition and format/document-class detection;
- staged preview before canonical mutation when review is materially useful, with high-confidence mechanically validated paths permitted to auto-admit under the rules below;
- source-level provenance and content identity/fingerprint;
- preservation of original source artifacts where useful and rights-permitted;
- idempotent/repeat import, overlapping-source detection, and duplicate detection;
- bounded parsing/resource controls;
- mapping into candidate semantic records rather than direct uncontrolled writes;
- staged cleaning, field mapping, normalization, clustering, transformation, and reconciliation where useful;
- reusable transformation/reconciliation/parser recipes with structural signatures, preview/effect summary where material, provenance, versioning, and undo/replay semantics where proportionate;
- candidate entity-resolution suggestions that preserve ambiguity rather than silently fuzzy-merging distinct people/accounts/assets/places/records;
- source/profile drift detection when a previously learned format or invariant changes;
- confidence/evidence classification sufficient to distinguish auto-admitted, provisional, review-required, and rejected/unsupported extraction results;
- import diagnostics, omissions, partial status, and retry;
- revocation/deletion behavior appropriate to copied versus externally referenced source data.

OMN-CAP-011: Ambiguous cleaning, deduplication, entity resolution, or source reconciliation SHALL remain reviewable and SHALL NOT silently destroy, merge, overwrite, or upgrade canonical meaning. Original source identity plus the accepted transformation/reconciliation lineage SHALL remain inspectable enough to explain how imported records were produced.

OMN-CAP-019: Acquire/Ingest SHOULD maximize autonomous throughput. A repeated or structurally recognized source MAY bypass routine human preview when the selected parser/profile is identity-bound, its required invariants pass, no material drift is detected, and downstream canonicalization/reconciliation gates support the interpretation. Material ambiguity or failed invariants SHALL route only the affected items to Triage/Clarify rather than block unrelated valid records.

OMN-CAP-020: Confirmed import corrections SHOULD be reusable as scoped transformation/parser rules so subsequent equivalent sources require less or no manual work. A learned profile SHALL bind enough source/account/document identity and structural evidence to detect incompatible format drift; a drifted source SHALL degrade to re-detection/review rather than silently applying stale offsets, columns, labels, or sign conventions.

Bulk data liberation is a first-class outcome. Repeated exports from the same source SHOULD be incrementally reconcilable rather than forcing full duplicate re-import. Projects such as HPI and Timelinize are reference patterns for broad personal-data acquisition; OpenRefine is a reference for reversible cleaning, clustering, reconciliation, transformation history, preview, and reusable recipes. Their runtime/storage assumptions are not automatically inherited.

## 5.2 Track / Observe

Reusable opt-in capture of user-defined observations, measurements, events, states, counters, timers, ratings, categorical values, and lightweight logs without requiring a bespoke domain module for every quantity.

Track/Observe SHOULD support, where useful:

- reusable tracker/observation definitions with stable identity;
- value type, unit, subject/entity, tags/context, note, and timestamp semantics;
- one-tap/low-friction entry and bounded timers;
- multiple views/references to one canonical tracker definition rather than duplicated writable trackers;
- derived formulas/functions and aggregations through Data/Analyze rather than hidden mutation of observations;
- recurrence/reminders through Time;
- charts through Visualize;
- optional import from Acquire/Ingest and approved sensors/connectors;
- export, correction, provenance, retention, and deletion appropriate to the owning domain.

OMN-CAP-004: Track/Observe SHALL remain voluntary. Absence of tracking SHALL NOT be treated as failure, neglect, deficiency, or permission for continuous monitoring. Generic trackers SHALL NOT bypass domain-specific safety, subject identity, provenance, privacy, or canonical-ownership requirements when observations are promoted into a specialist domain.

Track & Graph, Nomie, openScale, ActivityWatch, and comparable quantified-self systems are reference patterns for rapid capture, reusable tracker definitions, visualization, and observation history; their surveillance, native-runtime, engagement, or domain assumptions are not automatically inherited.

## 5.3 Data

Transform, validate, normalize, query, aggregate, units, schemas, forms/tables, generic calculations, and data mechanics without taking ownership of domain meaning.

Import mechanics that involve source acquisition/provenance live behind Acquire/Ingest; domain-specific canonicalization remains owned by the target domain.

## 5.4 Artifact

One shared artifact/document/blob service for:

- attachments and binary/text artifacts;
- content hashes and stable artifact identity;
- source/provenance and external references;
- import/export mediation;
- MIME/type validation;
- thumbnail/preview generation;
- text extraction/OCR where qualified;
- local derived transformations where justified, such as merge/split/page reorder, convert, compress, fill, watermark, redact, sign/mark, crop/rotate, or media trim/transcode;
- file metadata;
- retention/deletion/backup semantics.

OMN-CAP-013: Artifact transformations SHALL be explicit derived outputs by default. The original source SHALL remain available unless the user explicitly requests a destructive replacement and the owning workflow permits it. A visual signature/mark is not claimed as a legally qualified electronic/digital signature merely because Omnevum can render it; regulated/cryptographic signature claims require separately admitted authority and assurance.

Artifact handling SHALL NOT become a second semantic owner. Derived text, OCR, thumbnails, embeddings, and previews remain subordinate/rebuildable where possible.

Artifact SHOULD also support evidence-preserving Web/resource capture where justified, including source URL/metadata and bounded snapshots or standard archival representations such as extracted text, HTML, PDF, image/screenshot, or WARC-compatible artifacts when current browser/tooling and rights allow. Highlights/annotations remain separate user meaning linked to the preserved source. Readeck, wallabag, Linkwarden, ArchiveBox, Paperless-ngx, Karakeep, and comparable projects are reference sources for this lifecycle; Omnevum SHALL not inherit their server assumptions merely to gain preservation semantics.

Artifact/Acquire implementations SHOULD reuse qualified browser-native parsers, extractors, and transformation libraries rather than implementing file formats from scratch. Initial candidates include PDF.js for PDF parsing/rendering, pdf-lib/jsPDF or qualified successors for PDF creation/manipulation, MuPDF.js or a qualified successor where its higher-fidelity WASM PDF manipulation/output materially helps and its AGPL/source obligations fit the accepted distribution profile, Tesseract.js plus Scribe.js-class browser OCR/text-extraction/searchable-PDF tooling where local OCR is justified, unified/remark/rehype for structured text/Markdown pipelines, Mammoth.js or successor for DOCX extraction, PapaParse or equivalent streaming CSV parsing, SheetJS Community Edition or a qualified successor for spreadsheet interchange/data extraction, exifr or equivalent metadata extraction, fflate/zip.js-class libraries for bounded archives, Mediabunny/WebCodecs-class browser-native media parsing/encoding/transmux/transcode where target codec support permits, and FFmpeg-Wasm/ONNX/browser-image libraries only where broader codec/operation coverage justifies their larger runtime burden. LeafPDF, BrowserSnip, and DoxDock are current FOSS references for local-only document/media finishing workflows, worker isolation, source preservation, and operation registries. Exact choices remain currentness-qualified, and project source identity SHALL follow the canonical upstream rather than a stale mirror or package name when those diverge.

WARC/WACZ handling SHOULD evaluate current browser-oriented Webrecorder components such as warcio.js for streaming archive I/O and wabac.js/ReplayWeb.page for replay semantics before bespoke Web-archive parsing or rewriting. Replay machinery SHALL remain subordinate to Artifact and the one-service-worker rule: a library that normally installs its own service worker must be integrated into the platform-owned service worker/worker boundary, isolated on a separately qualified origin, or rejected for the baseline. Replayed archived content remains untrusted and SHALL NOT regain live-network, script, credential, canonical-write, or external-effect authority merely because replay fidelity benefits from URL rewriting.

Heavy parsing/OCR/inflation SHOULD run off the main interaction path in Workers where practical, expose cancellation/progress, and enforce input/inflation/resource bounds. Parser output is untrusted DERIVED data: rendered HTML or active content SHALL be sanitized/inert before presentation, and parse success SHALL NOT grant network, script, canonical-write, or external-effect authority. Where HTML sanitization is needed, Omnevum SHOULD use a qualified standards-based native Sanitizer API only on support-matrix rows that actually provide the required behavior, with a maintained FOSS sanitizer such as DOMPurify as an initial cross-browser fallback/comparator rather than bespoke regex filtering. Sanitizers are security-sensitive dependencies: configuration, browser-parser interactions, bypass advisories, Trusted Types/CSP interaction where available, and update currentness require dedicated adversarial fixtures and fast-lane handling.

Camera-to-document scanning SHALL compose Device/Input capture with Artifact transformations rather than create a separate scanner-owned document store. Before implementing bespoke edge detection, perspective correction, page ordering, filter, and PDF-export machinery, Omnevum SHOULD evaluate browser-local scanner FOSS and reusable primitives such as Nitidoc/OpenCV.js-class implementations. A strong default pattern is immutable source frames plus serializable/replayable edit recipes applied at preview/export time, with worker isolation, cancellation, manual-crop fallback, and explicit derived-output provenance. Whole-app scanner code remains license/currentness-qualified; algorithms/tests may be harvested independently when direct adoption has negative lifecycle value.

## 5.5 Visualize

Reusable visualization grammar for time series, distributions, comparisons, timelines, tables, progress, maps/geo layers, networks/relationships, calendars, and domain-specific projections.

One Omnevum visualization capability SHALL serve finance, fitness, projects, household, simulation, timeline, location, and future quantitative domains.

## 5.6 Analyze

Statistics, trends, correlations with causal caveats, change detection, anomaly discovery, comparisons, descriptive summaries, and data-quality checks.

OMN-CAP-008: For large tabular datasets, ad-hoc exploration, imported CSV/JSON/Parquet/Arrow data, or analytical workloads that materially benefit from SQL/columnar execution, Omnevum SHOULD evaluate browser-local analytical projection engines before implementing bespoke query machinery. DuckDB-Wasm is an initial high-priority candidate because it runs analytical SQL in the browser and supports Arrow, Parquet, CSV, and JSON without an application server. Perspective is a complementary candidate for high-performance in-browser pivots, streaming/user-configurable analytics, virtualized grids, and chart composition, while Arquero is a lighter JavaScript option for relational-style transformation of array/Arrow-backed tables.

An analytical engine SHALL remain a derived/query substrate over explicitly authorized snapshots/projections or imported analytical datasets. It SHALL NOT become a second canonical owner merely because SQL is convenient. Any engine database/cache may be rebuilt or migrated from canonical sources where technically feasible.

Accepted static-host/offline profiles SHALL self-host the required runtime/WASM/worker/extension assets or otherwise prove their availability under the declared offline envelope. Remote HTTP reads, dynamic extension downloads, or mutable CDN code SHALL NOT be silently activated by a query engine; they remain explicit Network/Artifact/Acquire effects subject to the normal egress, CORS, source, currentness, and integrity rules.

## 5.7 Model

Deterministic models, scenarios, Monte Carlo where justified, sensitivity analysis, sweeps, forecast comparison, calibration/backtesting where applicable, and model provenance.

## 5.8 Decide

Reusable decision mechanics including alternatives, criteria, assumptions, evidence, risks, stakeholders, reversibility, rationale, and review triggers. No mandatory universal weighted score.

## 5.9 Evidence

Source/provenance, currentness, authority, uncertainty, conflicting evidence, measurement/V&V relationships, source/reference identifiers, citation linkage, and traceability.

Bibliographic metadata, citation styles, formatted references, and bibliographies are derived presentations over source/reference records. Where research/reference workflows are admitted, Omnevum SHOULD reuse qualified CSL/CSL-JSON, BibTeX/BibLaTeX, RIS, Citation.js/citeproc-class tooling, or interoperable successors before implementing bespoke citation parsing/formatting. Changing a citation style SHALL NOT mutate the underlying source/evidence record.

## 5.10 History / Version

Reusable evolution semantics for user-meaningful records/configuration where change history provides justified value beyond current-state revision metadata or disaster backup.

History/Version MAY provide, proportionate to the owning domain:

- human-readable change history;
- before/after diff or field-level comparison;
- undo/revert to a prior valid revision;
- attribution/source/correlation of a change;
- named checkpoints or versions;
- branch/alternative exploration and merge where a real workflow earns it;
- migration/history compatibility;
- synchronization conflict context.

OMN-CAP-005: History/Version SHALL remain distinct from real-world Timeline and from Vault backup. It SHALL NOT create an alternate writable owner, silently retain deleted/sensitive content beyond the owning domain's declared retention/deletion policy, or require full immutable event sourcing/CRDT history for records that do not benefit from it. Canonical owners define what changes are historically meaningful and what may be pruned.

Where a selected substrate such as Automerge/Loro/Yjs/TinyBase provides useful history/diff/merge mechanics, Omnevum MAY expose them through this stable capability after privacy, storage-growth, migration, and portability qualification. Ink & Switch Patchwork/Upwelling and comparable local-first work are research references for user-facing version concepts rather than automatic runtime dependencies.

## 5.11 Reflect

Guided inquiry, journaling structures, retrospectives, and reflective workflows without diagnosis or personality profiling.

## 5.12 Assess

Quiz, competency, self-assessment, knowledge-check, and assessment mechanics subject to validity, rights, and privacy.

## 5.13 Progress

Protocols, progression, maintenance, repeated interventions, and progression rules.

## 5.14 Resolve

Structured perspectives, disagreement mapping, facilitation, and common-ground workflows.

## 5.15 Relate

People, stakeholder, entity, household/family, organization, and typed relationship mechanics. Relationship semantics SHALL not imply hidden scoring or ownership of other people.

Tags, folders, categories, collections, playlists/sets, smart collections, taxonomies, memberships, and saved groupings SHOULD reuse typed relationships plus Space/Scope and Compose/View projections where that preserves meaning. Omnevum SHALL NOT create a separate universal collection database merely because several domains expose grouping UX; domain-specific ordering, hierarchy, membership, and lifecycle rules remain with the owning semantics.

OMN-CAP-024: Relate + Analyze + Model + Evidence SHALL provide one reusable dependency/impact/synergy projection over authorized canonical records rather than each domain implementing its own hidden relationship engine. It SHOULD support upstream/downstream traversal, shared-resource contention, goal/outcome contribution, material change-impact analysis, common-bottleneck discovery, and candidate multi-outcome interventions. The projection is DERIVED: it SHALL preserve canonical ownership, source/provenance, uncertainty and temporal validity, and SHALL invalidate when controlling records, permissions, assumptions, relationship rules, or evidence change.

## 5.16 Time

Dates, durations, recurrence, reminders, timelines, temporal queries, schedules, and calendar integration without unnecessarily replacing the host/external calendar.

Time SHOULD prefer qualified standards implementations over bespoke calendrical arithmetic. Native Temporal MAY be used when the exact browser/support row provides the required behavior; because support is not assumed across all claimed targets, a qualified polyfill/fallback remains necessary where Temporal-dependent semantics are used. iCalendar/vCard parsing and recurrence SHOULD evaluate ICAL.js or an equally mature RFC-oriented browser library before implementing RRULE/time-zone recurrence logic by hand. Any recurrence library whose documented behavior intentionally differs from RFC 5545 requires explicit compatibility fixtures rather than being treated as standards-conformant by name alone. DST transitions, ambiguous/nonexistent local times, leap/calendar edge cases, time-zone database/currentness, recurrence expansion bounds, and import/export round trips SHALL be represented in Time qualification proportionate to the claim.

## 5.17 Place / Geo

Shared geospatial semantics for places, addresses, coordinates, regions, routes/tracks, visits, trips, distance, and map projections across travel, workouts, events, household assets, photos/artifacts, vehicle history, people, and environmental context.

Location data is potentially highly sensitive. Continuous/background tracking is NOT a core requirement and SHALL NOT be enabled merely because Place/Geo exists. Tracking/import connectors remain explicit, purpose-bounded, revocable, and target-qualified. Map/geocoding providers remain replaceable external capabilities where practical.

Before implementing a bespoke interactive map renderer, vector-tile stack, offline-map package format, or general-purpose client-side geometry library, Place/Geo SHOULD evaluate mature browser FOSS and open formats such as MapLibre GL JS plus PMTiles/Protomaps-compatible tooling and modular GeoJSON analysis libraries such as Turf. Offline map packages, glyphs, sprites, and rendered-tile caches are replaceable Artifact/cache assets linked to canonical Place/Geo meaning, not canonical place identity. Derived buffers, intersections, distances, classifications, simplifications, or other geometry results remain DERIVED unless explicitly adopted by an owning semantic command. Download scope, expected size, quota, source/currentness, attribution/license obligations, update/removal, and offline availability SHALL remain explicit; an OPFS-optimized route MAY accelerate large map packages but SHALL degrade honestly to a qualified alternative or PLATFORM_LIMITED state where the target lacks the required storage path.

## 5.18 Search

Local/federated authorized search over rebuildable indexes, including multilingual content, artifact text, timeline data, and geospatial filters where supported.

OMN-CAP-009: Before implementing a bespoke general-purpose search engine, Omnevum SHALL benchmark qualified browser-native FOSS against representative Vault sizes and supported mobile targets. Initial candidates include Orama for full-text/vector/hybrid/geospatial search, MiniSearch for compact memory-conscious full-text search, and FlexSearch for worker/persistent-index/document-search patterns.

Search implementation SHALL preserve source permissions/sensitivity and SHALL keep indexes, tokenization products, ranking state, vector embeddings, spelling/fuzzy structures, facets, and caches subordinate/rebuildable. Full-text search SHALL remain available without requiring embeddings or AI where practical. Vector/hybrid search MAY be added when it improves accepted outcomes, but generated embeddings remain derived sensitive state and do not expand the authorized corpus.

## 5.19 Share

Selective disclosure mechanics for explicit export/share outcomes.

Share SHALL distinguish at least:

- private local reference;
- copy/export;
- portable snapshot;
- read-only publication/projection;
- live collaborative state where separately admitted.

A reference SHALL NOT transitively disclose unrelated linked records, private overlays, credentials, AI authority, or external permissions. The static-host baseline MAY support file/snapshot-based sharing without a collaboration backend; live multi-user collaboration remains optional and separately qualified.

If live collaborative state is later admitted, access authority SHALL remain distinct from transport/provider ACLs and SHALL work under offline/concurrent conditions appropriate to the claim. Mature local-first access-control/encrypted-sync research and FOSS such as Keyhive/Beelay, MLS where applicable, Automerge/Yjs/Loro ecosystems, and qualified successors SHALL be evaluated before bespoke membership/key protocols.

For optional same-session/browser-to-browser collaboration, Omnevum SHOULD also evaluate qualified WebRTC/FOSS transports such as Trystero-class peer channels and relay-free QR/libp2p pairing where useful before adding a mandatory collaboration service. Peer discovery/signalling availability and metadata exposure, NAT/TURN/relay behavior, same-LAN/client-isolation limits, QR/out-of-band authentication semantics, room/link secrecy, end-to-end encryption, membership, revocation, persistence, and Recovery are separate concerns: a room identifier, QR payload, or successful peer connection SHALL NOT itself grant Share authority or canonical-write permission. Static-host peer transport is an optional transport optimization, not a replacement for Omnevum consent/Share policy or durable sync semantics.

OMN-CAP-017: Where Omnevum represents person-specific consent, delegation, membership, guardianship/caregiver authority, sharing grants, or revocation, that state SHALL be explicit, purpose/scope-bound, source/provenance-bearing, reviewable, and linked to the relevant subject/Space/Share policy. It SHALL NOT be inferred from a contact relationship, provider ACL, household label, possession of a link, AI suggestion, or presentation membership. Revocation/change SHALL invalidate future disclosure/effect authority and trigger reconciliation of affected derived/share state where applicable. Consent/grant records are policy/relationship evidence, not a second canonical owner of the underlying domain records.

OMN-CAP-023: Share SHALL provide a first-class local Context Export/Handoff projection for AI and other external analysis. From any authorized current selection, record, relationship neighborhood, saved view, Search result, Review, Space/Scope, or domain projection, the user SHALL be able to generate a bounded portable context without manually reconstructing or copy/pasting the underlying information. At minimum the export system SHALL support:

- Markdown (`.md`) and plain text (`.txt`) for prompt-ready human-readable context;
- CSV and TSV for semantically homogeneous tabular projections;
- JSON and JSONL for structured machine-readable context;
- a portable multi-file bundle when one flat format cannot preserve the selected structure without material loss.

A Context Export SHALL preserve or include, as applicable:

- export/profile identity and generation time;
- explicit purpose/task note when the user supplies one;
- source Space/Scope, selection/query/view identity, record types, time range, and currency/locale assumptions where relevant;
- stable record/source/artifact identifiers sufficient for traceability without exposing secret internal handles;
- provenance/source references, truth/evidence class, uncertainty/conflict/staleness, and last-verified time where material;
- relationships and ordering needed to preserve meaning;
- field/schema definitions or glossary where labels alone are ambiguous;
- assumptions and material derived/calculated status;
- an omission/redaction manifest when selected information is intentionally excluded, cannot fit the chosen representation, or exceeds a declared context budget.

Context Export SHALL be generated locally from the same authorized projection used by product views and the AI Context Broker. It SHALL NOT export credentials, reusable secrets, hidden permission state, raw AI/provider authority, unrelated linked records, or active/executable content. Imported source instructions remain untrusted data and SHALL be delimited/identified as source content rather than promoted to export-level instructions.

Format selection SHALL preserve semantics rather than merely file extension. CSV/TSV SHALL be used only for genuinely tabular rows or clearly separated tables and SHALL NOT silently flatten hierarchy, many-to-many relationships, conflicting values, or provenance into misleading columns. Markdown/text MAY use compact structured sections; JSON/JSONL and bundle formats SHOULD preserve richer typed structure. Any material representational loss SHALL be reported before export.

CSV/TSV or other spreadsheet-compatible Context Exports SHALL treat exported text as untrusted and SHALL be inert by default against formula/cell injection, active links, or equivalent spreadsheet-triggered behavior on the claimed consumer profile. Because no universal escaping scheme is safe for every spreadsheet and downstream parser, the export contract SHALL define and test the exact target interpretation, preserve the semantic source value through an inspectable safe representation or separate raw field where needed, and report any transformation that can affect round-trip data. A user-requested raw representation SHALL remain clearly distinguished from a spreadsheet-safe representation and SHALL NOT silently gain execution authority.

Context Export SHOULD support reusable declarative export profiles containing scope/query, format, field/detail level, artifact/excerpt inclusion, ordering, redaction rules, and context-size policy. Profiles are presentation/share configuration, not disclosure authority: each execution SHALL re-evaluate current access, sensitivity, source availability, and export consequences. Where useful, a prior export identity MAY be used to create a delta package containing material additions, changes, deletions/tombstones, invalidations, and unchanged-context references so recurring AI work does not require repeatedly exporting the entire context.

The export surface SHOULD show estimated byte/character/token-scale size before final handoff and SHOULD offer compact, standard, and full detail presets. If a declared size/context budget requires omission, compression, summarization, or sampling, the transformation SHALL be deterministic or provenance-bearing, preserve protected exact-value fields, label derived summaries, and produce an inspectable omission manifest. The system SHALL prefer removing irrelevant context over lossy compression of material evidence.

## 5.20 Compose / View

Reusable declarative composition of authorized records, projections, and capabilities into user- or AI-authored forms, tables, cards, dashboards, reports, charts, maps, timelines, capture templates, spatial canvases/diagrams, and focused workspaces without creating new canonical ownership.

Compose/View SHALL, where applicable:

- bind controls to stable semantic field/command identities rather than localized labels;
- derive validation from owning domain/schema contracts and never weaken those contracts;
- route writes through the same semantic command/action spine as hand-authored UI;
- preserve read/write/egress/permission boundaries independently of what a view displays;
- inherit platform theming, branding, localization, RTL, accessibility, responsive behavior, and Safe Presentation Mode;
- keep layouts/view definitions separately exportable, resettable, versionable, and recoverable from canonical domain data;
- permit user customization without requiring source-code forks;
- treat malformed/imported view definitions as untrusted declarative data, never executable authority.

OMN-CAP-006: A declarative view/schema MAY accelerate ordinary CRUD/capture/report UI, but specialized domain workflows SHALL remain hand-composable when generic generation would materially reduce usability, semantic clarity, performance, accessibility, or safety. Compose/View is a presentation/composition capability, not a universal writable database or low-code authority bypass.

Spatial/canvas views SHOULD reuse qualified browser FOSS such as Excalidraw, React Flow, Konva-class tooling, or a lighter successor instead of building generic pan/zoom/selection/shape/connector/serialization machinery from scratch. A standalone drawing/canvas MAY be an Artifact; when a canvas references canonical records, node position/style/grouping are view state unless an admitted semantic command explicitly changes the underlying record.

## 5.21 Automate

Bounded trigger/condition/action workflows designed around PWA background-execution limits. Correctness SHALL NOT depend on continuous browser background execution.

OMN-CAP-021: Automation SHALL prefer event-driven completion and deterministic catch-up over user-maintained routines. Relevant triggers MAY include import/capture completion, canonical mutation, open/resume, elapsed semantic due state, reconciliation opportunity, source/currentness change, or an explicitly configured rule. Where the browser cannot run while closed, the next safe open/resume SHALL reconcile missed work without pretending background execution occurred.

An automation MAY complete a reversible or provisional canonical action without a confirmation dialog only when the owning domain explicitly permits that action class, authority is current, predeclared evidence/rule/invariant gates pass, and the result remains inspectable and correctable. High-consequence external effects continue to use the normal command, confirmation, policy, and Effect/Outbox path.

## 5.23 Space / Scope

Reusable user-controlled context boundaries across otherwise independently owned records and capabilities. Examples may include Personal, Household, Work, Trip, Project, Emergency Binder, or an explicitly shared family/team context.

OMN-CAP-007: A Space/Scope SHALL be an overlay of references, defaults, views, and policy bindings over canonical records; it SHALL NOT become a second writable owner or duplicate a record merely because that record participates in more than one context.

Where useful, a Space/Scope MAY define or select:
- default lenses/views/navigation;
- bounded Search and Assistant context;
- attention/review filters;
- default Capture routing suggestions;
- sync/share/export destinations or policies;
- presentation/workspace preferences;
- member/subject visibility where an independently qualified permission/share model authorizes it.

A Space name, membership label, presentation profile, provider ACL, or transport group SHALL NOT itself grant data access, AI authority, disclosure authority, or canonical-write permission. Those effects continue to require the underlying record permissions, Share policy, sync/cryptographic membership, and semantic command path.

Deleting or leaving a Space SHALL NOT silently delete referenced canonical records unless the user separately authorizes the owning domain's deletion operation. A record MAY appear in multiple Spaces through stable references/projections without writable duplication.

Personal versus collaborative Spaces SHALL remain explicit. Live collaborative Spaces require the separately qualified Share, permission, sync/conflict, membership/revocation, and Recovery semantics; the local-only baseline may use Spaces purely for organization/context without any multi-user infrastructure.

Anytype personal/collaborative Spaces, AFFiNE workspaces/collections, SelfStore group/household patterns, and comparable local-first systems are reference sources for this boundary. Omnevum SHALL preserve its own canonical ownership and policy model rather than importing another product's space/account ontology wholesale.

## 5.24 Annotate / Markup

Reusable user- or AI-authored annotation semantics over authorized source material without silently modifying the source. Applicable targets may include text, Web captures, PDFs, images, document pages, map/geo features, timeline regions, audio/video time ranges, charts, and other Artifacts/projections where stable anchoring can be qualified.

Annotate/Markup SHOULD support, where useful:

- stable annotation identity, author/source, created/modified time, provenance, truth class, and applicable Space/Scope;
- highlights, comments, notes, tags, review marks, freeform ink/shapes, text/image regions, selectors/ranges, and links to semantic records;
- portable selectors/anchors with source artifact identity/fingerprint/version so annotations can detect source drift;
- explicit states such as CURRENT, STALE_TARGET, ORPHANED, AMBIGUOUS_REANCHOR, or UNSUPPORTED rather than silently moving an annotation when the source changes;
- export/import mappings to interoperable representations where available;
- History/Version for meaningful annotation edits and Evidence for source/citation relationships.

OMN-CAP-010: Annotation content SHALL remain separate canonical user/derived meaning linked to the target source unless the user explicitly requests a destructive/flattened/embed/export transformation. A highlight or AI note SHALL NOT mutate the source Artifact, upgrade source truth, or inherit disclosure authority merely because it is visually overlaid. Annotation sensitivity MAY exceed the source's sensitivity and SHALL be governed independently.

Where semantics fit, Omnevum SHOULD support or map to W3C Web Annotation Data Model/selectors rather than inventing proprietary anchoring/export structures. Hypothesis is a reference for Web/PDF text annotation and anchoring; Annotorious for image/region annotation; Zotero for separating source PDFs from annotation records; PDF.js and qualified browser PDF/canvas libraries may provide rendering surfaces. The archived Apache Annotator project MAY provide historical algorithm/tests/reference ideas but SHALL NOT be treated as a current maintained dependency without fresh qualification.

## 5.25 Triage / Clarify

Reusable bounded processing of captured, imported, suggested, or otherwise unresolved items whose semantic owner, actionability, relationship, duplicate/reconciliation state, or next disposition is not yet settled.

Triage/Clarify SHOULD preserve the original/raw item plus provenance while allowing proportionate outcomes such as:

- assign/confirm canonical owner and record type;
- convert or split one capture into one or more admitted semantic records/actions while preserving source linkage;
- link to an existing entity/project/person/Space/source rather than duplicate it;
- resolve an import/reconciliation/duplicate candidate;
- mark actionable versus reference/non-actionable;
- schedule, defer, delegate/wait, park for later, archive/reference, dismiss, or delete through the appropriate owning commands;
- accept/reject/edit deterministic or AI classification/extraction suggestions;
- batch only genuinely mechanical, homogeneous decisions with preview/undo where safe.

A triage queue is staging/operational state, not a universal task database. Resolved records continue to belong to their canonical owners, and a capture MAY bypass triage when the user or a safe explicit rule has already provided an unambiguous owner/action.

OMN-CAP-012: Triage/Clarify SHALL preserve ambiguity rather than fabricate certainty. AI classifications, priorities, extracted actions, and routing are proposals unless a user-authorized deterministic/reversible rule explicitly admits automatic handling. No unresolved input may be silently discarded, merged, escalated, published, disclosed, or promoted into high-consequence canonical meaning merely to empty an inbox or improve completion metrics.

OMN-CAP-022: Triage/Clarify SHALL be an exception surface, not a mandatory tollbooth. High-confidence mechanically validated items and previously confirmed scoped rules SHOULD resolve through their owning commands without routine user review; the queue SHOULD contain only unresolved, materially ambiguous, conflicting, low-confidence, or consequential cases plus explicitly user-inboxed items. Batch resolution is preferred for genuinely homogeneous exceptions.

GTD systems such as Trayzero, Mindwtr, Loop/weekly-review workflows, Super Productivity, and current task/planning FOSS are reference patterns for Capture -> Clarify -> Organize -> Engage -> Reflect. Paperless-ngx/document inboxes and personal-inbox/AI-triage projects provide classification/filing/proposal patterns. Omnevum generalizes only the triage mechanics; it SHALL NOT force every life domain into GTD/task semantics.

## 5.26 Effect / Outbox

One platform-owned product-runtime capability SHALL coordinate deferred, retryable, or outcome-uncertain external effects so individual modules and connectors do not invent incompatible offline queues, retry semantics, or completion authority.

Representative effects include connector mutations, calendar/contact writes, optional notification-companion sends, remote publication/share operations, uploads, external AI/tool effects, and other side effects that cannot be completed atomically with local canonical state.

OMN-CAP-014: A deferred/retryable external effect SHALL have one durable operation identity and owner, with enough typed state to preserve the originating semantic command/purpose, target adapter/destination, payload or stable payload reference, idempotency/reconciliation key where available, creation/expiry, current status, retry/backoff policy, current credential/capability handle reference rather than raw secret, result/remote identity, and evidence needed to reconcile an interrupted or ambiguous attempt.

The shared effect state SHOULD distinguish at least PENDING, IN_FLIGHT, SUCCEEDED, FAILED_RETRYABLE, FAILED_TERMINAL, CANCELLED, EXPIRED, and OUTCOME_UNKNOWN/RECONCILE where applicable. An adapter MAY use narrower internal states but SHALL map them to truthful platform semantics.

Before replay, the platform SHALL revalidate current purpose, authority, permission, Space/Scope, disclosure class, destination, credential/grant state, and data/schema compatibility. Revocation or changed authority SHALL block/cancel the affected future effect rather than replay stale authorization.

An ambiguous remote outcome SHALL be reconciled before blind retry when duplicate external consequences are materially possible. Omnevum SHALL NOT claim exactly-once delivery merely because it uses an idempotency key or retry queue; the contract SHALL state whether an effect is idempotent, deduplicated, reconciled, compensatable, or potentially duplicated.

Effects that are unsafe or meaningless to defer SHALL fail explicitly rather than being silently queued. Users SHALL be able to inspect material pending/failed effects and cancel/retry/resolve them where safe; failures that need attention SHALL flow through the one attention system.

Replay SHALL occur on open/resume/connectivity recovery and other safe opportunities. Background Sync, Periodic Background Sync, service-worker wakeups, or similar browser facilities MAY accelerate replay only on qualified targets and SHALL NOT be required for correctness.

Effect/Outbox is not a canonical life-domain owner, task list, sync-replica journal, or license to retain sensitive payloads indefinitely. Domain sync may maintain its own causal/replica metadata, but first-party modules SHALL NOT create a second generic external-effect authority.

Workbox Background Sync, TanStack-style paused-mutation patterns, IndexedDB outbox implementations, and offline-first systems with client-generated idempotent operation IDs are reference implementations/patterns to characterize before building bespoke queue mechanics; Omnevum retains the semantic/effect authority above any selected library.

## 5.27 Device / Input

One platform-owned Device/Input capability SHALL mediate user-gesture-, permission-, and hardware-bound browser/device input so modules do not independently reinvent camera, microphone, file/clipboard/share, location, scanner, or device-selection mechanics.

Representative sources include:

- camera/photo and user-selected image/video capture;
- microphone/audio recording and explicit voice capture;
- file and directory pickers where qualified;
- clipboard, paste, drag/drop, Web Share and Share Target inputs;
- geolocation and explicit place capture;
- barcode/QR scanning and other qualified camera-derived codes;
- screen/display capture only when explicitly invoked and justified;
- future sensors only after a concrete purpose, permission, privacy and support profile exists.

OMN-CAP-016: Device/Input SHALL expose capability/permission state, user gesture requirements, source/device selection where useful, start/stop/cancel lifecycle, resource ownership, and failure/degraded status through one broker. It SHALL NOT grant a module raw ambient hardware authority merely because another module previously obtained permission. Continuous/background camera, microphone, screen, location, or sensor capture is not a core requirement and SHALL NOT be enabled by default.

Captured meaning SHALL route to the appropriate existing capability/owner: bytes to Artifact/Acquire, observations to Track/Observe, location to Place/Geo, ambiguous scans to Triage/Clarify, and transcription/classification to DERIVED AI/Data output until accepted. Permission state, device IDs, stream handles and temporary capture buffers are technical/session state, not canonical life-domain meaning.

Native browser APIs SHOULD be used before wrappers. Qualified FOSS such as ZXing/browser barcode libraries, MediaRecorder/Web Audio helpers, image/metadata libraries, Kurumi/FormsOffline/field-data capture implementations, and later entrants MAY supply decoding, capture UX, device selection and test fixtures where they reduce lifecycle cost. Unsupported hardware paths SHALL retain file/paste/manual fallbacks wherever the user outcome can still be met.

OMN-CAP-018: Device/sensor acquisition SHALL distinguish at least DIRECT_WEB_API, USER_EXPORT_IMPORT, OPTIONAL_NATIVE_OR_LOCAL_COMPANION, EXTERNAL_STANDARD_CONNECTOR, and UNAVAILABLE/PLATFORM_LIMITED. Omnevum SHALL NOT claim direct browser access to native stores or sensors where the Web platform does not expose it. A user-export file/import route is a first-class path when it preserves the outcome without privileged native access; optional native/companion bridges MAY add continuous or richer access without becoming a core dependency. Health/wearable examples include Apple Health export ingestion, FHIR/other admitted health exports, and separately qualified HealthKit/Health Connect companions/native clients.

Large export ingestion SHOULD stream/aggregate incrementally where practical, preserve source provenance, support idempotent re-import/reconciliation, and avoid loading multi-hundred-megabyte archives wholly into memory on constrained mobile targets. Current FOSS such as Ygeia, Skulpt, Open Wearables and comparable projects are harvest/reference sources for export parsing, wearable normalization and native-bridge patterns; their medical authority, backend, or native-runtime assumptions are not inherited.

## 5.28 Platform capabilities

Persistence, files/artifacts, Device/Input, backup/recovery, permissions/policy, credential/key brokering, connectors/network, Effect/Outbox, attention/notifications, presentation/personalization, accessibility, localization, import/export, diagnostics, updates, health, package lifecycle, and qualified external effects.

OMN-CAP-003: New modules SHOULD consume these capabilities before adding private equivalents. A domain-specific implementation is justified only when the shared contract cannot preserve the domain's required semantics, performance, privacy, or lifecycle behavior.

# 6. Static-hosted PWA architecture

## 6.1 Hard hosting constraint

OMN-PWA-001: The complete non-optional Omnevum core SHALL be deployable and usable as a static HTTPS Web application on GitHub Pages or an equivalent free static host.

Normal core use SHALL NOT require:

- application server;
- server-side database;
- serverless function;
- LifeOS account service;
- proprietary backend;
- SSR;
- WebSocket server;
- mandatory hosted sync service;
- backend-held user secrets;
- required API proxy.

Any future capability that requires such infrastructure SHALL be optional, separately qualified, and SHALL NOT break the local static-host core when absent.

## 6.2 Browser-mode completeness

Installability is progressive enhancement. A normal supported browser session SHALL provide the complete core even where manifest-based installation is unavailable.

## 6.3 Hosting profiles

At minimum define:

- constrained free static host profile (GitHub Pages baseline);
- enhanced static host profile (optional stronger headers/configuration);
- self-hosted static HTTPS profile.

Core semantics SHALL remain the same.

## 6.4 Routing and base paths

Omnevum SHALL support:

- root-domain deployment;
- repository subpath deployment;
- custom domains;
- refresh/deep-link-safe routing without assuming server rewrites;
- service-worker scope under the configured base path;
- portable asset resolution.

## 6.5 One service worker

One platform-owned service worker SHALL own release/application asset caching and update/offline lifecycle. First-party modules SHALL NOT ship independent service workers.

Libraries whose normal integration assumes an additional same-origin service worker, including some Web-archive replay or offline toolkits, SHALL be adapted into the platform-owned worker boundary, isolated on a separately qualified origin, or rejected for the baseline. A dependency's use of a service worker is an implementation detail, not authority to create a second update/cache/network-control plane.

Canonical user records SHALL NOT use Cache API as their authoritative store.

## 6.6 No runtime upstream authority

A released Omnevum artifact SHALL continue to operate if development AI systems, package registries, upstream Git repositories, and build infrastructure are temporarily unavailable, subject only to explicitly optional external features.

No mutable third-party runtime CDN code SHALL be required in the accepted offline baseline.

## 6.7 Constrained static-host security profile

OMN-PWA-002: No core security, privacy, durability, or correctness claim SHALL depend on response headers, server middleware, edge functions, secrets, rewrite rules, or other controls unavailable on the constrained GitHub Pages baseline. Use browser/application controls that are actually enforceable on that profile and record their limits.

OMN-PWA-003: If a valuable optional capability requires stronger hosting controls (for example cross-origin isolation or host-set security headers that cannot be supplied by the baseline host), it SHALL be assigned to an enhanced/self-hosted profile, capability-detected, and fail/degrade explicitly without breaking baseline core operation. Omnevum SHALL NOT claim a header/control exists because it is desirable.

OMN-PWA-004: Static release assets SHALL be release-bound, integrity/version coherent, and self-hosted in the accepted artifact where required for offline/core operation. Runtime code SHALL NOT trust a mutable remote script merely because the source is reputable.

## 6.8 Fork/self-host portability

OMN-PWA-005: A qualified fork or rebrand SHALL be able to build and host the same core on an arbitrary compliant static HTTPS origin without source-level semantic changes. Origin-specific configuration (base path, OAuth public-client IDs, connector redirects, distribution branding, update origin) SHALL be isolated from canonical schemas/record IDs and supplied through documented build/runtime configuration rather than scattered constants.

# 8. Local storage and data integrity

## 8.1 Default storage model

Default implementation direction:

- IndexedDB: canonical structured records, indexes, metadata, migrations, journals.
- OPFS where qualified: large user-owned blobs/assets requiring file-like access.
- Cache API: rebuildable release/application assets only.
- Memory: transient or rebuildable working state.

The AI engineer SHALL validate current browser support, quotas, persistence behavior, performance, and library choices before freezing the implementation design.

## 8.2 Maximum practical browser storage

Omnevum SHALL request persistent origin storage where appropriate/supported, estimate available quota, expose usage and pressure, and use as much safe browser-local capacity as legitimately available without inventing a fixed low application ceiling.

Browser persistence remains fallible. Off-origin backup remains the device/origin-loss control.

## 8.3 Pressure and reclamation

Under pressure, reclaim safe rebuildable caches/indexes/downloaded model assets before canonical state where useful.

Canonical records SHALL NOT be silently deleted to preserve replaceable assets.

Quota exhaustion SHALL fail explicitly and preserve export/Recovery paths where technically possible.

## 8.4 Transactions and crash consistency

Canonical writes SHALL use transaction/commit boundaries that preserve invariants after crash/interruption. Cross-store operations (for example IndexedDB plus OPFS) SHALL use a recoverable journal, content-addressed commit, or equivalent design proportionate to risk.

## 8.5 Concurrency

Multiple tabs/windows shall use explicit revision/concurrency semantics. Universal silent last-write-wins over conflicting semantic meaning is prohibited.

Stale/incompatible clients SHALL be fenced from unsafe writes and enter reload/reconcile/read-only behavior as appropriate.

## 8.6 Corruption handling

If canonical or important inherited state fails schema, decode, integrity, migration, or consistency validation, Omnevum SHALL NOT silently substitute empty/default state and overwrite the suspect source.

Preserve original bytes/state where feasible, enter explicit READ_ONLY/DEGRADED/RECOVERY posture, and resume writes only after verified repair, migration, restoration, or explicit informed reset.

## 8.7 Storage backend portability and fallback

OMN-DATA-003: Omnevum SHALL abstract canonical persistence behind stable data/storage contracts so supported browser rows are not coupled to one optional browser storage mechanism or library. OPFS use SHALL be capability-qualified; loss/unavailability of OPFS SHALL NOT by itself make ordinary structured core records inaccessible when an admitted IndexedDB-backed path can satisfy the requirement. Large-artifact features MAY degrade explicitly when the target cannot provide an equivalent safe capacity/performance path.

OMN-DATA-004: Storage/library replacement SHALL preserve canonical semantic identity, transactional/migration invariants, export/Recovery, and test evidence. A database library is implementation infrastructure, not the owner of Omnevum meaning.

## 8.8 Optional remote-backed artifact tier

OMN-DATA-005: To reduce local-capacity anxiety for large user artifacts, Omnevum MAY support an explicit remote-backed/cold artifact policy in which canonical metadata, provenance, content identity/hash, and availability state remain locally represented while a verified provider/Vault copy supplies the artifact payload on demand. Omnevum SHALL NOT silently evict the last unique local payload until a current user policy permits it and the remote copy has been verified complete/integrity-valid; remote-only artifacts SHALL expose their offline unavailability and Recovery dependency.

# 9. Vault, backup, restore, and sync

## 9.1 Omnevum Vault

Omnevum SHALL define an open, documented, versioned portable representation used for backup, restore, provider migration, and optional synchronization.

A portable Vault shall contain, as applicable:

- manifest/schema version;
- canonical records in open documented serialization;
- relationships and provenance;
- domain/package ownership;
- tombstones where needed;
- content-addressed or manifest-bound blobs;
- integrity digests;
- migration/version metadata;
- optional encryption metadata.

Portable identity SHALL NOT depend on OS path conventions, browser-private database internals, case sensitivity, Unicode normalization quirks, or user-visible filenames.

## 9.2 Backup is not sync

Backup = coherent recovery snapshot.

Sync = ongoing reconciliation among replicas/devices.

The UX, implementation, evidence, and failure semantics SHALL keep these concepts distinct.

## 9.3 Off-origin backup

Device/origin-loss Recovery SHALL be claimed only when a current backup exists outside the protected origin/device.

Large backup/export SHALL support bounded-memory streaming/chunking where practical.

Restore SHALL validate completeness, integrity, schema compatibility, migration plan, destination capacity, and conflicts before commit and SHOULD support dry-run/preview plus rollback/LKG or transactional compensation proportionate to risk.

## 9.4 Optional encryption

Encrypted backup/sync SHALL use mature reviewed standard cryptography and FOSS implementations where available. Bespoke cryptographic protocol design requires a documented unmet requirement and independent assurance.

Browser WebCrypto SHOULD be preferred where its standardized primitives and target support satisfy the accepted protocol. Where an admitted reviewed protocol requires primitives or streaming constructions not adequately supplied by the qualified WebCrypto profile, the engineering system SHOULD evaluate mature browser FOSS such as libsodium.js before implementing cryptographic primitives or wrappers. A low-level crypto library does not authorize invention of a new protocol; exact algorithms, parameters, key lifecycle, serialization, interoperability, and recovery remain specification- and evidence-bound.

The user SHALL be warned that lost encryption credentials may make encrypted recovery impossible.

## 9.5 Provider-neutral replication

Cloud/self-hosted storage is an interchangeable transport, not the canonical semantic database.

Initial target transports SHOULD include, subject to current browser/API qualification:

- Google Drive;
- Microsoft OneDrive;
- WebDAV;
- Nextcloud via appropriate WebDAV/API paths;
- Dropbox;
- local/manual file export/import.

Advanced/optional transports MAY include S3-compatible object storage and additional providers.

Provider IDs, object IDs, and remote paths SHALL NOT become canonical record identity.

## 9.6 Browser-auth connector rules

Browser/public-client OAuth/OIDC connectors SHALL use provider-current recommended public-client flows and protections such as authorization-code + PKCE, exact redirect matching, state/nonce where applicable, least scopes, bounded token lifecycle, explicit revocation, and no embedded client secret.

Connector tokens SHALL remain broker-controlled and SHALL NOT be exposed to ordinary modules or model-visible context.

Before implementing generic OAuth 2.x/OIDC browser-client state, PKCE, callback/session, token-lifecycle, discovery, or protocol plumbing from scratch, Omnevum SHOULD evaluate maintained standards-oriented FOSS such as oidc-client-ts or a qualified successor. The library remains an implementation adapter: provider-specific public-client registration, redirect/CORS rules, scopes, token/storage policy, revocation, proof-of-possession support, and provider terms remain Omnevum/provider qualification concerns, and unsupported/obsolete grants SHALL NOT be enabled merely because a library exposes them.

## 9.7 WebDAV/self-hosted baseline

WebDAV SHOULD be the baseline open personal-storage protocol for self-hosted file-oriented Vault transports where browser/CORS constraints permit.

Connector preflight SHALL report explicit states such as AVAILABLE, AUTH_REQUIRED, CORS_BLOCKED, DEGRADED, or UNSUPPORTED instead of pretending every WebDAV host is browser-compatible.

## 9.8 Sync semantics

Sync SHALL remain optional; local-only core operation must remain complete.

Sync conflict semantics SHALL be owned by the relevant domain/data contract. Universal silent last-write-wins is prohibited for conflicting canonical meaning.

Remote sync input is untrusted. Validate schema, version, authorization, integrity, causal/revision state, and tombstone/deletion semantics.

Deletion SHALL define tombstone, retention, garbage collection, stale-replica, and rejoin behavior so old replicas cannot silently resurrect confirmed deletions.

## 9.9 Background execution limitation

Correctness SHALL NOT depend on continuous browser execution, timers, or indefinite service-worker survival.

Sync SHALL reconcile on open/resume and at safe opportunities; background mechanisms are enhancements, not correctness prerequisites.

## 9.10 Static-host OAuth and connector deployment independence

OMN-SYNC-001: Consumer-cloud connectors SHALL NOT require an Omnevum token broker or secret-bearing backend merely to operate from the official static distribution. Where provider policy permits, the official distribution MAY embed non-secret public client identifiers registered for its exact origins.

OMN-SYNC-002: Forks, personal static-host deployments, and rebranded distributions SHALL have a documented way to supply their own provider public-client identifiers/redirect origins (or another provider-current browser-public-client mechanism) without modifying canonical schemas or depending on the official Omnevum origin. Client secrets SHALL NOT be embedded in the static bundle.

OMN-SYNC-003: If a provider cannot support a qualified browser/public-client path for an arbitrary deployment without a secret backend, that route SHALL be optional/PLATFORM_LIMITED for that deployment rather than becoming a core dependency. At least one user-operable zero-required-payment backup/sync path that does not require an Omnevum-operated backend SHALL remain available in the completed sync profile (for example qualified WebDAV/self-hosted storage and portable manual Vault transfer).

## 9.11 Scalable Vault repository mode

OMN-SYNC-004: The portable single-archive Vault SHALL remain the simplest baseline backup/transfer format. For larger or frequently updated Vaults, Omnevum SHOULD additionally qualify a transport-neutral repository mode when evidence shows meaningful storage/bandwidth/recovery benefit.

A scalable repository mode MAY use mature patterns demonstrated by restic, Kopia, rclone, and similar FOSS, including:

- immutable/coherent snapshot manifests;
- content-addressed blobs/chunks;
- incremental upload;
- deduplication and optional compression;
- client-side authenticated encryption for untrusted remote storage;
- bounded-memory streaming;
- resumable interrupted transfer;
- integrity verification/scrub;
- retention/pruning and garbage collection with reachability safety;
- provider-neutral object naming and repository identity.

OMN-SYNC-005: Chunking, deduplication, encryption, and repository garbage collection SHALL NOT become hidden alternate semantic ownership. Canonical meaning remains in Omnevum records/artifacts/manifests, and a repository optimization SHALL be replaceable/migratable without changing canonical IDs. Tiny Vaults SHALL NOT be forced through complex repository machinery when a coherent portable archive is simpler.

The engineering system MAY reuse qualified FOSS algorithms/formats/libraries directly where browser/static-host compatible and license-compliant; desktop-native backup binaries are design/behavior references or optional companions unless separately adapted and qualified for the Web runtime.

## 9.12 External sync/backup companions and open protocols

Optional user-controlled companions such as Syncthing or rclone MAY synchronize/export user-selected Vault files or repositories outside the browser when the user deliberately configures that workflow. They SHALL NOT become prerequisites for core operation, and Omnevum SHALL distinguish browser-controlled success from external-companion state it cannot directly prove.

Open protocols/formats SHOULD be preferred where they reduce vendor lock-in, including WebDAV, CalDAV/iCalendar/VTODO, CardDAV/vCard, FHIR/SMART-on-FHIR or qualified health schemas, GeoJSON/GPX, OPDS, WARC, OFX/QIF/CSV and successors where applicable. Protocol support SHALL remain capability/target/currentness qualified rather than inferred from format names. Radicale, Nextcloud, Open mHealth and comparable FOSS may provide reference implementations and interoperability tests.

## 9.13 Open personal-data endpoints

Omnevum SHOULD support a provider-neutral class of user-controlled personal-data endpoints in addition to vendor file/object transports when a qualified open protocol reduces lock-in or enables cross-application portability.

Candidate families include remoteStorage-compatible endpoints, Solid Pods, Etebase/EteSync-style end-to-end-encrypted application data stores, and future qualified personal-data-store/DWN-style protocols. These are interoperability candidates, not automatic canonical stores.

OMN-SYNC-006: Local Omnevum canonical state SHALL remain usable and authoritative for the local-first core unless a separately admitted domain explicitly delegates ownership to an external authoritative source. Connecting a Pod, remoteStorage account, Etebase collection, or similar endpoint SHALL NOT silently make its provider schema the Omnevum ontology or change canonical IDs.

OMN-SYNC-007: Personal-data endpoint adapters SHALL expose the same provider-neutral connection, health, conflict, revocation, migration, and Recovery semantics as other replication transports where those semantics apply. Unsupported browser CORS/auth/background behavior SHALL degrade honestly.

## 9.14 Browser-native Vault/sync substrate reuse gate

Before implementing a bespoke general-purpose Vault backup/sync/merge engine, the engineering system SHALL characterize current browser-native FOSS that already satisfies substantial parts of the requirement and compare direct adoption, adapter wrapping, component harvest, and bespoke implementation.

`selfstoredev/selfstore` is a current high-priority candidate because the inspected project provides a browser IndexedDB working copy, documented portable ZIP backup format with independent reader/test vectors, optional authenticated encryption, files, typed/headless status, Google Drive/WebDAV/file targets, pluggable targets, multi-device merge strategies, and state `gather/apply` integration without requiring an application server. `remotestorage/remotestorage.js` is a high-priority open personal-storage candidate because it combines browser-local data with remoteStorage servers and optional consumer-cloud synchronization. Etebase/EteSync, Solid-compatible clients, and later entrants remain comparators.

The MPES does NOT preselect any candidate. Qualification SHALL verify exact current source/license/maturity, static-host/browser support, cryptographic implementation, backup-format fit, domain-specific conflict semantics, tombstones, large-Vault behavior, artifact handling, portability, migration, Recovery, accessibility of user flows, and update/replacement cost.

A library default such as global last-write-wins SHALL NOT override Omnevum's domain-owned conflict rules merely because it is convenient. If an existing backup/container specification satisfies Omnevum's portability, integrity, encryption, versioning, rights, and cross-platform requirements, extending/adopting that qualified format is preferred to inventing a new container solely for project ownership.

# 10. AI architecture

## 10.1 AI is optional

Removing all user-facing AI runtimes/providers SHALL leave canonical data, deterministic Home/reviews, Search, Visualize, Model, Decide, Evidence, backup/export/restore/Recovery, and ordinary package lifecycle semantically intact.

## 10.2 Shared AI broker

All first-party AI usage SHALL go through one provider-neutral broker.

Representative operations:

- summarize;
- extract;
- compare;
- classify;
- explain;
- draft;
- suggest;
- translate;
- surface relationships;
- assist search/retrieval.

Apps/modules SHALL NOT receive raw provider credentials.

## 10.3 Context broker

Each AI request SHALL declare:

- purpose;
- authorized source/context projection;
- operation;
- destination/route;
- disclosure class;
- expected output contract.

No module or Assistant session receives ambient access to the complete personal state.

OMN-AI-011: Manual AI handoff SHALL use the same bounded Context Broker projection and Share/Context Export controls as a connected AI route. Omnevum SHALL be able to generate a prompt-ready context package from the current authorized task/Space/selection without requiring the user to reconstruct background, copy individual fields, or re-explain already recorded decisions. The package SHALL separate system-generated framing, user-authored objective/instructions, canonical facts, source material, assumptions, derived analysis, unresolved conflicts, and omitted/redacted content so an external AI can distinguish instruction from evidence. Exporting context does not grant the recipient AI write authority, Omnevum permissions, credentials, or access to omitted Vault state.

OMN-AI-012: AI-context packaging SHALL optimize useful information per context cost without hiding evidence limits. When a target context size is known or selected, the Context Broker SHOULD prioritize governing constraints, current task state, material evidence, decisions, assumptions, open questions, exact-value fields, and source references before secondary history or redundant prose. Repeated or derivable information SHOULD be compressed or referenced once; contradictory evidence SHALL remain represented rather than collapsed into a synthetic certainty. A generated compact context is DERIVED and SHALL remain traceable to the fuller authorized source projection.

## 10.4 Routes

Possible routes:

- browser-native/local inference;
- explicitly downloaded browser model;
- official subscription-backed agent/SDK/CLI/client route where current provider terms permit the requested use;
- reverse integration in which a subscribed external assistant is granted bounded access to Omnevum through an admitted standard/app/connector;
- LAN/self-hosted endpoint;
- metered cloud API/provider;
- manual handoff to an external assistant using a bounded Context Export/Handoff package;
- none.

Route selection SHALL consider privacy/egress, task capability, target support, memory/thermal/storage burden, latency, marginal cost, subscription/quota state, availability, and current provider terms.

Hidden route widening/fallback is prohibited. A zero-marginal-cost or subscription-included route SHALL NOT silently fall through to metered API/usage-credit billing without explicit user policy.

## 10.5 Terms-Aware Subscription AI Bridge

Omnevum SHOULD let users reuse AI capability they already legitimately pay for whenever a provider exposes a supported subscription-authenticated agent, SDK, CLI, app, connector, or other integration surface suitable for the requested task. The goal is minimum marginal user cost without converting a consumer website into an unofficial API.

OMN-AI-004: Subscription reuse SHALL be provider-, plan-, surface-, task-scope-, version-, region-, and terms-aware. The Currentness Register SHALL distinguish at least SUPPORTED_SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTE, SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITS, REVERSE_INTEGRATION_ONLY, MANUAL_HANDOFF_ONLY, API_ONLY, TERMS_BLOCKED, UNKNOWN, and STALE/RECHECK_REQUIRED. A route's presence in an older Omnevum release is not proof that it remains permitted.

OMN-AI-005: Omnevum SHALL NOT harvest browser session cookies, passwords, refresh tokens, private web endpoints, or another product's OAuth credentials to impersonate an allowed client. Browser/DOM automation, headless-browser control, request replay, user-agent imitation, or similar techniques MAY be considered only when the exact provider's current terms/documentation explicitly permit that automation for the user's account and task. Omnevum SHALL NOT bypass CAPTCHAs, anti-bot systems, rate/usage limits, client restrictions, protective measures, or identity checks. A provider rule prohibiting programmatic extraction, third-party OAuth piggybacking, or client misrepresentation makes that route TERMS_BLOCKED rather than an engineering challenge to evade.

OMN-AI-006: AI-route cost state SHALL be explicit enough to prevent surprise charges. Useful classes include LOCAL_NO_MARGINAL_COST, SUBSCRIPTION_INCLUDED, FREE_QUOTA, METERED_CREDIT, METERED_API, and UNKNOWN. Hitting a plan allowance SHALL stop/degrade, change to another pre-authorized no-cost route, or ask according to user policy; it SHALL NOT silently buy credits, enable usage-based billing, or switch to an API key.

OMN-AI-007: A separately installed local Subscription Bridge Companion MAY be used where the provider's supported interface is native/CLI/SDK rather than browser-callable. The core PWA remains complete without it. The companion SHALL use the provider's official authentication flow/client or documented SDK; keep reusable vendor credentials outside ordinary PWA/module/model context; bind any local bridge to a qualified loopback/native-messaging boundary with origin/caller authentication and least authority; expose typed operations/status rather than raw credential/session access; and remain independently disableable, updateable, auditable, and replaceable.

OMN-AI-008: Reverse integration is a first-class alternative. Where a subscribed external assistant can officially call Apps/MCP/connectors/skills or another admitted interface, Omnevum MAY expose an explicitly bounded read/fetch/action surface so the user's existing assistant can work with Omnevum. The same semantic-command, Space/Scope, permission, disclosure, Effect/Outbox and provenance controls apply; provider plan limitations such as read-only versus write-capable integrations remain explicit.

OMN-AI-009: "Ambient AI" means that an authorized route can be selected as a shared default across Omnevum surfaces and invoked opportunistically by admitted workflows; it does NOT grant continuous whole-Vault access, continuous sensor monitoring, hidden background prompting, or unrestricted proactive disclosure. Every invocation still receives purpose-bounded context through the Context Broker.

Initial 2026-09-17 currentness snapshot, to be revalidated before implementation/release:

- Anthropic Claude Pro/Max: official Claude Code subscription authentication is supported; Anthropic currently states that Claude Agent SDK, `claude -p`, and third-party Agent-SDK app use still draw from subscription limits for now. This is the strongest current candidate for a general subscription-backed Omnevum companion, subject to exact current terms and SDK behavior. Anthropic separately prohibits third-party tools that misrepresent identity or attempt to route traffic against subscription limits.
- OpenAI ChatGPT Plus/Pro: Codex is included with eligible ChatGPT plans and official Codex clients can use plan allowance when signed in with ChatGPT. This is a high-value engineering/technical route, not evidence that the ordinary ChatGPT consumer web UI is a third-party API. Current OpenAI consumer terms prohibit automatic/programmatic extraction of ChatGPT data/output, so consumer-web scraping/DOM extraction is TERMS_BLOCKED unless terms change. Reverse ChatGPT Apps/MCP access is plan/workspace dependent and may be read/fetch-only on some plans.
- Google AI Pro/Ultra: consumer developer allowance has moved from Gemini CLI to the official Antigravity CLI for individual plans. Google explicitly prohibits third-party software from harvesting/piggybacking the legacy Gemini CLI OAuth path. Antigravity's official headless/structured modes may be used for supported agent/development workflows when current terms permit, but Google consumer subscription identity SHALL NOT be repurposed as a hidden generic Gemini API credential.

The durable requirement is the route framework and compliance behavior, not the above vendor snapshot. Provider terms, product names, entitlements and supported automation surfaces are volatile currentness data.

When a provider explicitly permits browser-based automation for the relevant user/account/task, Omnevum MAY use a user-controlled browser automation bridge (for example a qualified Playwright/browser-use/open-browser-use-class adapter) as a subscription route. Such a bridge remains subject to the same Context Broker, credential broker, cost/currentness, disclosure, rate-limit and Effect/Outbox rules and SHALL identify itself truthfully where required. The mere technical ability to imitate a browser, reuse cookies, call private endpoints, or suppress automation detection is not evidence of permission.

## 10.6 Local AI

Opening an optional local-AI surface SHALL NOT silently trigger a material model download. Before substantial transfer/storage commitment, identify the model/runtime/source, approximate footprint where known, license/use constraints, metered/cost exposure, cancellation, and reclamation behavior.

Downloaded models/embeddings are replaceable subordinate assets and SHOULD yield to canonical data under storage pressure.

OMN-AI-010: Before implementing model loading, tokenization, WebGPU/WASM execution, multimodal preprocessing, model caching, streaming generation, embeddings, speech recognition/synthesis, or local tool-call protocol machinery from scratch, Omnevum SHALL evaluate mature browser-native FOSS/runtime primitives. Initial candidates include Transformers.js + ONNX Runtime Web, MLC WebLLM, wllama/llama.cpp-WASM class runtimes, and qualified browser built-in AI APIs where portability/privacy/currentness are acceptable. Speech-specific candidates SHOULD include whisper.cpp WASM and sherpa-onnx WebAssembly for local transcription/voice processing and Kokoro.js/Transformers.js-class routes for local synthesis where their exact model/runtime/license/support profile fits. LocalMode/TinyWhale/WebML-class projects may provide integration/test patterns. Runtime/model choice SHALL remain adapter-based and capability-detected.

Heavy local inference SHOULD run off the main UI path, expose download/warm-up/progress/cancellation/resource state, respect memory/thermal/storage limits, and fail/degrade cleanly on unsupported WebGPU/WASM targets. Model/runtime files are untrusted replaceable assets; model-specific executable/custom code SHALL NOT gain ambient browser authority merely because it accompanies weights.

## 10.7 AI provenance and truth

Material retained AI output SHALL preserve bounded provenance including route, provider/runtime, strongest exposed model/version or explicit managed/unknown state, execution time, and authorized source identities where useful.

AI output remains DERIVED unless independently established otherwise.

## 10.8 Retrieval

If RAG/federated retrieval is admitted, qualify authorized source set, chunking/index/update/invalidation, sensitivity/currentness, grounding/source references, stale-source fallback, prompt-injection defenses, privacy/egress, and failure mode.

Indexes/embeddings remain rebuildable projections under source permissions.

## 10.9 AI tool use and future agents

AI-generated action/tool requests are untrusted proposals. They SHALL traverse the same typed command, permission, validation, sink, reconciliation, and provenance path as equivalent non-AI actions.

Product AI maturity SHOULD progress deliberately:

1. assistive AI;
2. contextual AI;
3. bounded tool use;
4. bounded multi-step workflows;
5. constrained proactive preparation.

No maturity stage permits AI to become an alternate canonical authority.

## 10.10 Open AI/tool interoperability

OMN-AI-003: Before building bespoke external AI-tool/resource connector protocols, Omnevum SHOULD evaluate current open interoperable standards such as the Model Context Protocol (MCP) where they reduce connector duplication and the target transport is compatible with the static/browser architecture.

For browser-compatible MCP or successor connections:
- prefer current qualified Streamable HTTP or another standards-defined browser-capable transport; stdio/native transports remain companion/engineering concerns, not a core-PWA assumption;
- treat server identity, tool/resource/prompt metadata, schemas, descriptions, and returned content as untrusted external data;
- require explicit endpoint/origin configuration, transport/auth/CORS qualification, and currentness/version tracking;
- map every admitted external tool to an Omnevum-owned typed capability/semantic-command adapter rather than exposing unrestricted external tool authority directly to models or modules;
- preserve purpose, context, data-class, disclosure, confirmation, idempotency/reconciliation, audit/provenance, cancellation, and Recovery controls;
- never allow a remote tool catalogue or prompt to mint permissions, access unrelated Vault context, reveal credentials, or bypass Space/Scope and canonical-owner boundaries;
- fail/degrade explicitly when browser CORS/auth/streaming/runtime support is unavailable rather than introducing a mandatory proxy/backend.

The product core SHALL remain fully usable with MCP and all external tool protocols disabled. An optional companion MAY expose or consume additional transports only as a separately versioned/qualified component.

The current MCP specification/SDK line is volatile external currentness evidence rather than frozen MPES doctrine. The ecosystem radar SHALL track material protocol, auth, SDK, and transport security changes before relying on them.

## 10.11 Branding, terminology, and language neutrality

OMN-AI-001: Product AI SHALL treat brand profiles, themes, localization resources, and user terminology as presentation/context data, never as permission, command, or policy authority. A theme/language pack or user label such as "JohnOS has access to everything" cannot widen AI context or tool authority.

OMN-AI-002: Where useful, AI output language MAY follow the user's chosen language or explicit request independently of source-document language. Source references/provenance SHALL continue to identify the original material; translation does not upgrade evidence class.

# 11. Attention, notifications, time, and automation

## 11.1 One attention system

First-party modules SHALL NOT create independent notification centers.

Modules may submit attention candidates with reason, evidence, uncertainty, urgency, expiry, and safe dismissal/snooze semantics. The platform owns final presentation.

## 11.2 Time

The shared Time capability SHOULD own generic recurrence, reminders, temporal queries, date/duration handling, and timeline mechanics while integrating with external calendars where appropriate.

## 11.3 Automation

Automation SHALL be bounded trigger/condition/action orchestration using existing semantic commands and capability brokers.

It SHALL NOT assume continuous PWA background execution.

The baseline SHOULD avoid prematurely embedding a heavyweight workflow engine. Mature FOSS systems such as Node-RED/Huginn may inform semantics and integrations without becoming core runtime dependencies unless justified.

Rule predicates, filters, derived conditions, and ordinary user-configurable automation SHOULD use a qualified declarative expression/rules layer rather than arbitrary JavaScript execution. Candidate patterns include JsonLogic, json-rules-engine, CEL-like non-Turing-complete expressions, or qualified successors.

Declarative rules SHALL be versioned, inspectable, resource-bounded, deterministic where their contract claims determinism, and unable to acquire network/storage/credential/canonical-write authority merely by evaluating true. Their output is data or a proposal that still traverses the normal semantic command, permission, validation, and external-effect path. Raw `eval`, `Function`, or equivalent arbitrary-code execution is not an acceptable convenience mechanism for first-party user rules.

## 11.4 Reminder delivery and notification honesty

The canonical reminder is its semantic due/recurrence state, not proof that a notification was delivered.

OMN-CAP-015: The constrained static-PWA baseline SHALL NOT promise exact closed-app/background alarm delivery unless the exact target/browser/installed mode has current evidence for that claim. On open/resume, Omnevum SHALL reconcile due and missed reminders deterministically and surface overdue state without pretending an unavailable notification fired.

Where useful, delivery classes SHOULD be explicit, for example:
- IN_APP: surfaced while Omnevum is active;
- ACTIVE_NOTIFICATION: browser/OS notification while the app/service worker is demonstrably able to deliver;
- OPPORTUNISTIC_BACKGROUND: best-effort qualified background delivery with no exact-time guarantee;
- EXTERNAL_COMPANION: delivery delegated to a separately authorized external calendar/notification service or companion;
- UNAVAILABLE/PLATFORM_LIMITED: semantic reminder retained but this delivery route is not supported.

Notification permission denial, browser suspension, service-worker termination, or unavailable background APIs SHALL NOT delete, complete, or invalidate the reminder itself.

Stronger optional delivery MAY integrate with external calendars, OS/host capabilities, or user-controlled notification services such as qualified ntfy/Gotify-class endpoints. Such routes are optional external effects using the normal disclosure/credential/Effect-Outbox controls; no required Omnevum push server enters the static-core baseline.

Background Sync, Periodic Background Sync, Web Push, Notification Triggers, Badging, and related APIs SHALL be capability/currentness tested rather than assumed from browser name. Exact timing, persistence, battery optimization, permissions, and installed-mode limitations SHALL be reflected honestly in the Support Matrix.

Kurumi, FlowBlock, FullCalendar-class tools, Loop Habit Tracker, ntfy/Gotify and comparable systems are reference sources for scheduling/reminder UX and optional delivery profiles; their server/native assumptions are not automatically inherited.

# 12. App and game platform

## 12.1 Platform rule

Omnevum shall evolve into a platform where new first-party apps and games are primarily compositions of shared capabilities plus domain-specific semantics and UX.

The dependency direction is:

app/game -> public Omnevum SDK/contracts -> capability brokers -> platform runtime

Never:

- app -> platform internals;
- app -> another app's private canonical store;
- app -> raw provider credentials;
- app -> independent service worker;
- app -> global shell ownership;
- app -> independent permission/notification/backup/AI/recovery systems.

## 12.2 Package manifest

Each substantive package declares, at minimum:

- stable package ID/version;
- public display identity;
- trust class;
- framework/API compatibility;
- entrypoint(s);
- owned canonical records;
- consumed/provided semantic commands;
- required/optional capabilities;
- permissions/external effects;
- data schema/migrations;
- import/export/backup behavior;
- offline/degraded behavior;
- health/Recovery contract;
- responsive/input/accessibility profile;
- update/rollback/uninstall/retirement behavior.

## 12.3 Package archetypes

The framework SHOULD be proven against materially different archetypes such as:

- stateless app;
- stateful app;
- artifact/document app;
- model/simulation app;
- game.

## 12.4 Games

Games share the same package, security, update, storage, localization, personalization, accessibility, recovery, and release systems as apps, with game-specific capabilities where needed:

- save/load;
- pause/resume;
- deterministic seed/replay where useful;
- input abstraction;
- rendering/context-loss handling;
- frame/memory/battery budgets;
- asset lifecycle;
- game-state migration.

Games are also platform stress tests for performance, suspension/resume, input, assets, and lifecycle correctness.

OMN-GAME-001: Before implementing generic 2D rendering, scene, asset, animation, input, camera, collision/physics, or game-loop infrastructure, the game factory SHALL evaluate mature browser-native FOSS engines/libraries and adopt or adapter-wrap the lowest-lifecycle-cost qualified option. Phaser 4 is an initial primary candidate; Excalibur, KAPLAY, PixiJS and later entrants are comparators. Omnevum SHALL own the package/save/permissions/accessibility/Recovery contracts, while engine-specific mechanics remain behind a replaceable game-runtime adapter where practical.

A game-engine update is an upstream candidate subject to the same characterization, migration/save compatibility, browser/mobile, performance, security, licensing, rollback, and exact-candidate gates as other material dependencies.

## 12.5 Extension sequencing

Initial ecosystem: first-party modules only.

Later phases may admit:

- declarative templates;
- themes;
- language packs;
- schemas;
- dashboards/views;
- automation recipes;
- bounded skills;
- only after a real isolation model, sandboxed executable third-party extensions.

Arbitrary remote trusted-code loading and same-origin untrusted plugins are out of baseline scope.

OMN-APP-002: Before designing a bespoke executable-extension sandbox, Phase 8 SHALL evaluate mature confinement/capability-security approaches such as SES/Endo/Hardened JavaScript, LavaMoat-style capability policies, sandboxed cross-origin iframes/workers, WebAssembly/QuickJS-class isolated runtimes, Extism-class host-controlled WASM plug-ins, quickjs-emscripten-class embedded interpreters, or qualified successors. No candidate is accepted merely because it uses a Worker, iframe, WebAssembly, or an embedded interpreter: the threat model SHALL test authority leakage, prototype/global escape, host-function/capability leakage, storage/network access, CPU/memory denial, message validation, update/supply-chain behavior, and revocation. Cross-origin-isolation/SharedArrayBuffer requirements SHALL remain enhanced-profile constraints rather than silently entering the GitHub Pages baseline. If a robust isolation model is not proven, executable third-party packages remain out of scope while declarative extensions continue.

## 12.6 Schema-driven scaffolding and UI acceleration

OMN-APP-001: The app factory SHALL avoid hand-building repetitive forms, tables, filtering, validation wiring, dashboard layout, and ordinary record editors when a qualified schema-driven/headless FOSS composition can generate or supply the same outcome behind Omnevum contracts.

Before bespoke generic CRUD/view infrastructure, evaluate current candidates such as:

- JSON Forms and/or React JSON Schema Form for schema/UI-schema-driven forms;
- TanStack Form/Table/Virtual or qualified headless equivalents for typed forms, data grids, and large lists;
- GridStack or qualified equivalents for responsive user-configurable dashboard layout;
- Radix UI, React Aria, or qualified accessible primitive libraries for interaction primitives;
- existing NeumanOS components and other adopted-launchpad UI where they satisfy Omnevum contracts with less lifecycle cost.

Generated/schema-driven UI SHALL inherit Omnevum semantic IDs, canonical commands, permissions, validation, theme tokens, personalization, localization/RTL, accessibility, responsive constraints, and Recovery behavior. A JSON Schema/UI schema or generated renderer is presentation/configuration data and SHALL NOT become authority to write fields or invoke commands outside the owning domain contract.

The generator SHOULD be able to create a safe baseline record-list/detail/form/capture experience from package/domain metadata, while allowing specialized workflows to replace generic presentation without forking the data or capability model. Generated-owned and user-owned customization SHALL remain distinguishable for updates.

## 12.7 Commodity editor and office-component reuse

Omnevum SHALL preserve the non-goal of rebuilding commodity office software merely for feature parity. When a document, spreadsheet, presentation, diagram, or whiteboard workflow materially improves Omnevum and can be supplied by an embeddable FOSS component, reuse/adaptation is preferred to bespoke editor-engine development.

OMN-APP-003: Before implementing substantial rich-text/document layout, spreadsheet formula/grid, presentation-canvas, or generic whiteboard mechanics, evaluate qualified embeddable FOSS such as Univer, Excalidraw, Lexical/TipTap/Milkdown/ProseMirror-class editors, browser-only OnlyOffice/WASM projects, Omnitext-class reusable format editors, InjOffice-class surgical OOXML/WASM components, and current successors. An editor MAY own its document-format internals as an Artifact or package-owned canonical record, but SHALL NOT become a second universal life-data store, permission system, notification center, AI broker, sync owner, or global shell. Heavy editors SHOULD be optional/lazy-loaded so the mobile-first core does not pay their resource cost when unused.

Static/browser-only office candidates are particularly valuable because they can preserve the no-required-backend invariant while supporting DOCX/XLSX/PPTX-class workflows. Qualification SHALL verify exact component license/trademark/notice obligations, isolation boundary, first-load/offline asset size, memory/mobile behavior, worker/WASM requirements, unsupported-format refusal/loss semantics, and whether host-owned save/provenance can remain under Artifact. An AGPL or trademark-bearing editor is not rejected merely for its license, but its distribution/source/branding obligations must be satisfied before adoption.

Import/export round-trip claims require exact-format tests and loss reporting. If high-fidelity editing is better delegated to an external office application, Omnevum SHOULD integrate/open/export rather than absorb the entire office stack.

# 13. FOSS-first capability acquisition and upstream lifecycle

## 13.1 Governing acquisition mandate

OMN-FOSS-001: No material subsystem SHALL be implemented from scratch until current browser/platform capability, existing Omnevum capability, open standards, owner code, mature maintained FOSS, narrow adaptation, and compositional reuse have been evaluated.

OMN-FOSS-002: The engineering system SHALL aggressively maximize qualified reuse of relevant FOSS and owner code to minimize time-to-MVP and time-to-accepted-full-scope completion. It MAY incorporate, adapt, fork, wrap, transplant, merge, port, refactor, or otherwise reuse FOSS code where applicable rights permit and where whole-lifecycle value is positive.

OMN-FOSS-003: Omnevum targets a coherent capability superset, not a literal codebase union. Reuse SHALL NOT waive security, privacy, accessibility, rights/license, currentness, Recovery, static-host/mobile constraints, performance, updateability, or replaceability.

OMN-FOSS-004: Open source is not a trust level. Imported FOSS source, packages, build scripts, migrations, assets, tests, release artifacts, and documentation are candidate inputs that earn Omnevum trust only through provenance, rights, architecture, security, test, target, and lifecycle qualification.

## 13.2 Capability dispositions

Every materially relevant capability discovered in the owner portfolio or reference ecosystem SHALL receive one or more explicit dispositions:

- ADOPT: use qualified code/subsystem substantially intact;
- ADAPT: modify or wrap qualified code;
- INTEGRATE: keep the system external and connect through an API/protocol/format;
- EXTRACT: reuse algorithms, schemas, tests, workflows, data formats, or information architecture;
- GENERALIZE: promote the underlying mechanic into a broader shared Omnevum capability;
- MERGE: consolidate overlapping implementations into one canonical Omnevum implementation;
- REIMPLEMENT: recreate the capability where code/runtime/license/integration reuse is unsuitable but the outcome remains justified;
- DEFER: valuable but currently sequenced later with an explicit trigger/dependency;
- REJECT: net-negative, redundant, incompatible, unsafe, rights-blocked, or outside the accepted product boundary.

A high-value low-cost qualified capability SHOULD default toward ADOPT/ADAPT/GENERALIZE rather than DEFER. DEFER SHALL NOT be used as a convenience label to make the capability-superset claim appear complete.

## 13.3 High-speed transplantation contract

Speed-oriented reuse SHALL preserve architectural coherence.

When adopting/cannibalizing an existing project:

1. identify the exact useful capability rather than importing the whole upstream stack by default;
2. characterize existing behavior and retain useful upstream tests/fixtures where rights permit;
3. isolate or remove server/native/runtime assumptions incompatible with the static PWA unless the external system is intentionally retained as an optional companion;
4. map canonical data ownership before writes are admitted;
5. route storage, files, AI, network, notifications, permissions, sync, theming, localization, and Recovery through Omnevum shared contracts instead of preserving duplicate authorities;
6. preserve attribution, notices, and upstream lineage;
7. add characterization/contract tests before or alongside semantic refactor;
8. keep the initial transplant as small as practical, then generalize only when repeated consumers justify it;
9. verify responsive/accessibility/security behavior even if upstream did not;
10. remove superseded duplicate machinery once parity and migration are proven.

A fast transplant MAY temporarily retain an upstream internal module boundary behind an Omnevum adapter when that is the shortest safe path. Temporary architecture debt requires an owner, tests, removal/recheck trigger, and shall not leak as a new public platform contract by accident.

## 13.4 Stable adapter seams

Omnevum modules SHALL depend on Omnevum contracts, not unstable upstream internals.

Examples:

- Visualize contract -> renderer adapter -> ECharts/Vega-Lite or successor.
- Local persistence contract -> TinyBase/Dexie/RxDB/PGlite/SQLite-WASM/native adapter as qualified.
- CRDT/sync-merge contract -> Automerge/Yjs/TinyBase or successor where justified.
- Sync transport contract -> Google Drive / OneDrive / WebDAV / Dropbox or successor.
- AI broker -> browser-local / LAN / cloud provider adapters.
- Artifact parser/OCR contract -> qualified FOSS parser/extractor adapters.
- Place/Geo contract -> provider-neutral mapping/geocoding/import adapters.

A material upstream replacement SHOULD require changes inside the adapter/boundary rather than ordinary domain modules when semantic compatibility is preserved.

## 13.5 Capability Coverage Matrix

OMN-FOSS-005: Maintain a machine-readable Capability Coverage Matrix across the accepted reference corpus.

For each project/capability, record at least:

- project/source and exact inspected identity/date;
- FOSS versus source-available/proprietary/comparator status;
- license/rights state;
- maintenance/maturity/currentness;
- material capability;
- target Omnevum capability/domain;
- disposition;
- implementation/integration source;
- coverage state;
- intentional omission/defer reason;
- tests/evidence;
- upstream-update path;
- replacement/removal trigger.

## 13.9 Patch, fork, and vendor topology

Choose the lowest-burden route that preserves speed and updateability. Preferred patterns include:

1. exact pinned package/dependency with no local patch;
2. configuration or public extension point;
3. stable Omnevum adapter around upstream;
4. vendored exact upstream snapshot plus clearly separated local patch layer;
5. maintained fork with recorded upstream remote/base and small reviewable delta;
6. clean Omnevum reimplementation using behavior/tests/standards where direct reuse has negative lifecycle value.

This order is guidance, not dogma: a maintained fork MAY be faster and safer than a contorted adapter if the evidence supports it.

Every maintained patch/fork delta SHALL have:

- purpose;
- upstream base/range;
- touched capability;
- regression/characterization evidence;
- conflict risk;
- upstream issue/PR reference where useful;
- removal/upstreaming trigger.

OMN-FOSS-008: Local patches SHOULD be upstreamed when doing so reduces recurring maintenance burden and is compatible with project goals/rights, but Omnevum SHALL remain independently buildable even if upstream declines or disappears.

## 13.10 License, rights, and code-adoption posture

The owner explicitly authorizes use of any FOSS project whose license obligations can be satisfied. Omnevum SHALL be license-neutral in technical selection: permissive, weak-copyleft, and strong-copyleft options may all be evaluated for best whole-lifecycle value.

The engineering system SHALL:

- verify the exact license for the exact adopted version;
- preserve copyright/notices/source-offer or corresponding-source obligations as applicable;
- maintain component-level license/provenance information;
- detect material upstream license changes;
- evaluate whether direct combination changes distribution/source obligations;
- use process/API/protocol isolation, alternative FOSS, or clean reimplementation when that yields a better compliant architecture;
- never assume "public on GitHub", "source available", AI-generated, or visually similar means reusable under FOSS rights.

Source-available/proprietary projects MAY be used as product/comparison evidence to identify needs and capability gaps, but code/assets SHALL NOT be copied without applicable rights.

Where a copyleft adoption implies a material distribution-license change, the engineering system SHALL choose a compliant architecture under the owner's broad FOSS-adoption direction when a clear lawful route exists; irreducible legal ambiguity remains a real external gate, not a reason to silently violate terms.

## 13.11 Runtime and reconstruction independence

An accepted Omnevum release SHALL NOT require upstream repositories, package registries, vendor build infrastructure, or discovery services to remain online for normal local operation.

Where legally/practically possible, accepted releases SHOULD retain exact reconstruction inputs or content-addressed source/package artifacts sufficient to rebuild after yanking/disappearance. A missing non-retainable input narrows the reconstruction claim explicitly.

- Fireproof as a comparative browser-embedded document database/content-addressed encrypted replication design whose cryptographic-consistency, sync, portability, backup, migration, long-term maintenance and threat-model claims require independent qualification before any canonical-state role;
- SQLSync as a comparative Apache-2.0 collaborative offline-first SQLite wrapper when a structured collaborative record/file genuinely benefits from reactive SQL plus eventual replication; it SHALL NOT make live collaboration or its edge/network assumptions mandatory for local-only domains;

OPFS/SQLite implementations that require cross-origin-isolation response headers incompatible with the GitHub Pages baseline SHALL NOT silently become the only canonical path. Header-independent VFS modes, IndexedDB fallback, or another qualified substrate must preserve the baseline support contract. Current wa-sqlite evidence demonstrates multiple IndexedDB/OPFS VFS strategies without a universal COOP/COEP requirement; exact durability, concurrency, import/export transparency, browser support, and performance still require Omnevum benchmarking rather than being inferred from the library feature list.

No one substrate is selected merely because it combines the most features. Canonical ownership, browser portability, crash consistency, migration, backup, storage pressure, bundle/resource cost, static-host support, sync requirements, and replaceability control the decision.

## 13.14 Whole-application launchpad evaluation

OMN-FOSS-010: Before committing Phase 0 to a greenfield application shell, the engineering system SHALL characterize at least one current whole-application FOSS candidate that materially overlaps Omnevum's hard constraints and compare three routes: fork/adapt as launchpad, harvest substantial components, or greenfield shell plus selective reuse.

- static-host/GitHub-Pages compatibility;
- mobile-first/responsive/accessibility fit;
- local-first data ownership and migration burden;
- backend/account assumptions;
- PWA/offline/update behavior;
- module/capability modularity;
- personalization/i18n architecture;
- test/CI quality;
- security/privacy/supply-chain posture;
- license and upstream-update burden;
- amount of useful working functionality preserved;
- estimated time to MVP and full accepted scope;
- cost of removing conflicting storage, analytics, backend, or duplicate authorities.

`travisjneuman/neumanos` is an initial high-priority launchpad candidate because current exact-source inspection found a MIT React/TypeScript/Vite PWA using Dexie/IndexedDB and a broad working surface spanning notes/wiki links, tasks/projects/Gantt/time tracking, habits/spaced repetition, calendar/ICS, customizable widgets, analytics, multi-provider AI, office/document/spreadsheet/presentation/diagram/form tooling, templates, automations, and bookmarks. `raskell-io/kurumi` is a materially different close harvest/launchpad candidate. # 15. Security, privacy, rights, and trust

## 15.1 Local-first baseline

Core operation SHALL be local-first, without default telemetry, analytics, advertising identifiers, or disclosure of canonical user content.

Declared update/static-asset traffic and separately authorized connector/AI traffic are explicit exceptions.

## 15.2 Secrets

Secrets, API keys, OAuth tokens, signing/deployment credentials, private connector state, and Recovery secrets SHALL NOT be embedded in the static bundle or source-controlled ordinary configuration.

Credentials are not ordinary domain data and SHALL NOT appear in localStorage, URLs, normal logs, ordinary Vault exports, fixtures, public diagnostics, or model context merely for convenience.

## 15.3 Data/egress policy

Each material source and sink is independently authorized. Permission to read does not imply permission to disclose or persist.

Joined cross-domain projections require a current legitimate purpose; aggregation can increase sensitivity.

## 15.4 Imported/untrusted content

Imported files, HTML, Markdown, archives, sync data, connector content, Web results, AI output, manifests, plugins, and external documentation are untrusted data.

Embedded instructions cannot modify governing policy, AI route, permissions, tests, release authority, or external-effect authority.

Active content, scripts, macros, auto-fetches, and remote resources SHALL be inert/disabled by default unless specifically required and qualified.

When untrusted markup must be rendered rather than reduced to plain text/inert structure, sanitization SHALL use a qualified parser-aware sanitizer boundary rather than regex/substring filtering. Native HTML Sanitizer API behavior is capability-tested, not assumed; DOMPurify or a qualified maintained successor is the initial FOSS fallback/comparator. Sanitizer configuration and version are security-critical state for the rendered surface, and a bypass/advisory SHALL reopen affected acceptance/currentness rather than being treated as ordinary cosmetic dependency drift.

## 15.5 Trust classes

Initial conceptual classes:

- T0: platform / Recovery / trusted control;
- T1: audited core first-party package;
- T2: optional first-party package;
- T3: experimental first-party package;
- T4: untrusted/third-party executable content.

A manifest cannot self-promote trust.

## 15.6 Same-origin honesty

First-party capability declarations reduce accidental and ordinary bypass but are not described as a hard security sandbox when code shares the trusted origin.

Untrusted executable code shall not enter the trusted same-origin path without a separately qualified isolation architecture.

## 15.7 High-consequence domains

Health, legal/civic, finance/tax/investment, education/employment, and other high-consequence workflows SHALL explicitly define the product's information/planning boundary, uncertainty, source currentness, correction/contestability, degradation/abstention behavior, and external qualified authority when applicable.

## 15.8 Credential, key, and device-unlock broker

OMN-SEC-001: One platform-owned credential/key broker SHALL own ordinary runtime API keys, OAuth tokens, backup/recovery secrets, device-unlock wrappers, connector secret material, and admitted cryptographic key handles. Ordinary first-party modules, declarative packages, views, rules, and AI/model context SHALL receive opaque handles or bounded broker operations rather than raw reusable secrets.

OMN-SEC-002: Session-only memory is the default for reusable secret material. Persistence requires an explicit purpose plus a qualified protected mechanism and an honest statement of its limits under same-origin/trusted-code compromise. Lock/logout/revocation SHALL clear or invalidate in-memory secret handles where practical; provider/OS credential removal and Omnevum-local enrolment removal are distinct operations and SHALL be represented truthfully.

OMN-SEC-003: Passkeys/WebAuthn MAY be used as an optional device-local unlock/wrapping mechanism only after runtime capability testing of the exact required extension/behavior. Where WebAuthn PRF or a comparable authenticator-bound secret is used, the preferred pattern is to wrap an independently portable recovery/backup secret rather than make the authenticator credential the sole data-decryption authority.

Passkey/device unlock SHALL fail closed and SHALL remain optional. Loss of a device/passkey/browser profile, unsupported PRF capability, or migration to a different Omnevum HTTPS origin/RP ID SHALL NOT make portable user data unrecoverable when the user retains the documented recovery secret/key. Rehosting/origin migration SHALL provide a tested path to unlock through the portable recovery route and re-enroll a new local device wrapper.

OMN-SEC-004: Cryptographic identity, recovery identity, device identity, provider identity, Space membership, branding identity, and human/person records are distinct concepts. A convenient device/passkey enrolment SHALL NOT silently become account ownership, sharing authority, or canonical subject identity.

Key/password/passkey rotation, revocation, expiry where applicable, recovery/export rules, and currentness SHALL be explicit. Ordinary Vault exports SHALL exclude reusable connector credentials by default; any intentionally exported recovery/key material SHALL use a separately declared protected recovery workflow.

SelfStore's current passkey/WebAuthn-PRF implementation is a reference pattern because it wraps an existing portable secret and deliberately preserves password recovery; WebAuthn/FIDO guidance and qualified FOSS such as open-passkey/other audited implementations SHALL be evaluated before bespoke authenticator/key-wrapping code. Project-specific code is candidate evidence, not automatic security assurance.

# 16. Health, reliability, fault tolerance, and self-healing

## 16.1 Health states

The platform and substantive modules SHALL expose cheap explicit health states such as:

- HEALTHY
- DEGRADED
- FAILED
- RECOVERING
- SAFE_MODE
- CANONICAL_DATA_RISK
- REBUILDABLE_STATE_FAILURE
- OPTIONAL_CAPABILITY_FAILURE

Workflow state, health, visibility, configuration, volatile session state, and diagnostics SHALL remain logically distinct.

## 16.2 Failure containment

Failure of AI, sync, search, visualization, one module, one connector, or other optional capability SHALL NOT take down unrelated core operation.

## 16.3 Deterministic self-healing

Self-healing means bounded repair of unambiguous rebuildable technical state, for example:

- rebuild search indexes;
- clear/recreate caches;
- restart a worker;
- reacquire optional assets;
- regenerate derived projections;
- safely retry idempotent/reconciled sync operations;
- repair known deterministic migrations.

Self-healing SHALL NOT guess, invent, overwrite, or "repair" ambiguous canonical user meaning merely to restore a green status.

Repeated recurrence after repair SHALL escalate from automatic reconciliation to diagnosis.

## 16.4 Fault injection

Release qualification SHALL intentionally exercise applicable failures such as:

- tab/browser crash;
- service-worker failure;
- suspension/freeze/resume;
- stale tab/client;
- storage pressure/quota exhaustion;
- corrupt rebuildable state;
- interrupted update;
- interrupted migration;
- unavailable AI/provider;
- unavailable sync provider/network;
- conflicting replica;
- malformed import;
- module failure;
- invalid customization/theme;
- expired/revoked permission or OAuth state.

## 16.5 Mission-critical claim

"Mission-critical" is a bounded Web/PWA assurance claim, not regulated safety certification.

Before using that label, the release SHALL identify critical functions, credible hazards/consequences, operating envelope, degradation/fallback/Recovery criteria, dependency risks, observation/escalation, browser/platform limits, backup assumptions, RPO/RTO objectives where meaningful, and materially independent assurance proportionate to consequence.

If the gate is not closed, use the lower assurance level actually supported.

# 17. Recovery and Safe Mode

Recovery SHALL remain reachable independently of a failed module wherever technically possible.

Recovery capabilities SHOULD include:

- inspect release/storage/health;
- export retained state;
- restore backup;
- enter Safe Mode;
- enter Safe Presentation Mode;
- disable a failing package;
- clear rebuildable caches/indexes;
- inspect/rollback/repair update state;
- repair/re-run migration;
- reset presentation without deleting canonical data;
- export bounded privacy-minimized diagnostics.

Boot failure SHALL NOT automatically destroy/reset canonical user data.

Uninstalling code, disabling a package, deleting package state, deleting user records, purging backups, and retiring a domain are distinct operations.

OMN-OPS-001: Engineering currentness SHOULD be automated where it reduces delay and maintenance burden, but automated discovery never implies automated acceptance.

Suitable qualified engineering-plane mechanisms may include:

- Renovate or equivalent dependency-update automation for candidate PRs/changelogs;
- OSV-Scanner and/or equivalent vulnerability matching;
- Syft or equivalent SBOM generation;
- Grype or equivalent SBOM/dependency vulnerability analysis;
- zizmor or equivalent GitHub Actions/static CI workflow analysis;
- OpenSSF Scorecard as an external project-security posture signal/comparator, not sole trust evidence;
- GitHub security advisories/releases/API;
- selfh.st/apps release feeds and other curated FOSS discovery feeds;
- repository/package-registry release metadata;
- custom bounded scripts only where existing tools cannot supply the needed signal.

Tool choice is currentness-sensitive and SHALL be revalidated rather than permanently hard-coded by this MPES.

## 19.3 Candidate queues

Maintain logically distinct queues where useful:

- SECURITY: exploitable/advisory changes requiring expedited triage;
- DEPENDENCY: routine package/tool updates;
- UPSTREAM_FEATURE: new features/fixes in adopted projects;
- NEW_ENTRANT: newly discovered projects that may outperform or fill gaps;
- PLATFORM_STANDARD: browser/API/standard changes;
- LICENSE_EOL: licensing, maintainer, archive, or EOL changes;
- SUBSCRIPTION_ROUTE: provider-plan/terms/auth/surface changes that can enable, narrow, meter, or prohibit an AI subscription route.

A queue item SHALL carry source identity/currentness, affected capability, urgency/consequence, initial disposition, and the exact trigger for deeper evaluation.

## 19.4 Security fast lane

A security candidate may bypass normal feature batching but SHALL NOT bypass:

- source/provenance verification;
- affected-scope analysis;
- regression testing;
- data/migration safety;
- Recovery/rollback or forward-repair planning;
- exact-candidate release evidence.

If a safe patch is not immediately available, Omnevum SHOULD disable, isolate, or degrade only the affected optional capability where possible rather than leaving known exposure active or unnecessarily taking down unrelated core functions.

## 19.5 New-entrant and upstream-feature reassessment

Before a material release and at justified maintenance checkpoints, the engineering system SHALL ask:

- Has a newer project made a bespoke Omnevum subsystem unnecessary?
- Has an adopted upstream added a feature that lets Omnevum delete local code?
- Has a new FOSS implementation materially improved browser/PWA fit, accessibility, performance, security, localization, or sync?
- Has a project become abandoned, compromised, relicensed, or hard to maintain?
- Has a new open standard made a proprietary/provider-specific adapter unnecessary?
- Does a new entrant close a Capability Coverage Matrix gap at lower lifecycle cost?

Positive answers create candidate maintenance work; they do not silently mutate the released runtime.

The engineering system SHOULD monitor currentness event/lifecycle driven rather than through wasteful continuous polling. Product runtime does not need an ecosystem-radar daemon.

## 20.1 Canonical lifecycle

Every material product/platform/app/game scope follows:

INTAKE

-> STAKEHOLDER + SYSTEM CONTEXT
-> CAPABILITY GAP + CURRENT RESEARCH / FOSS HARVEST
-> REQUIREMENTS
-> SDD / ADR / THREAT MODEL
-> ACCEPTANCE + TEST DESIGN
-> CHARACTERIZE EXISTING/ADOPTED BEHAVIOR WHERE APPLICABLE
-> RED
-> GREEN
-> REFACTOR / CONSOLIDATE
-> PROVE
-> INTEGRATE
-> PACKAGE
-> RELEASE CANDIDATE
-> REPRODUCIBLE BUILD / PROVENANCE / SBOM
-> DEPLOY / SMOKE / ROLLBACK PROOF
-> RELEASE
-> OPERATE / OBSERVE
-> DIAGNOSE / REPAIR / MAINTAIN / UPDATE
-> DEPRECATE
-> RETIRE / ARCHIVE

Natural-language vibe coding is the high-speed input interface. It is not an exemption from specification, traceability, tests, security, accessibility, rights, release integrity, Recovery, or lifecycle evidence.

A feature request SHALL NOT jump directly from prompt to bespoke implementation when an existing capability/FOSS/integration can satisfy the underlying outcome.

## 20.2 AI engineering mandate

The AI engineer is expected to behave as a capable senior principal engineer plus product/system architect, security engineer, test engineer, release engineer, FOSS integrator, accessibility engineer, maintainer, and ecosystem scout, while spawning specialists when useful.

The AI SHALL:

- use current authoritative research when material;
- perform XY/outcome analysis on material solution-shaped requests;
- maximize qualified use of existing owner code, FOSS, standards, and interoperable protocols;
- continuously compare Omnevum's capability gaps against current FOSS/new entrants;
- prefer code acquisition/consolidation to reinvention when lifecycle-positive;
- choose safe professional defaults instead of asking routine questions;
- think creatively and out of the box;
- run cheap disconfirming experiments early;
- characterize inherited/adopted behavior before risky refactor;
- replace failed approaches rather than looping blindly;
- delete superseded duplicate code when a stronger shared/upstream capability is proven;
- minimize human effort and time-to-accepted-result;
- preserve long-term maintainability, updateability, user data, and static-host/mobile constraints while optimizing development velocity;
- continue until the accepted finite scope is complete or an actual irreducible gate remains.

Parallel FOSS research/harvest MAY proceed ahead of later product phase gates as nonshipping characterization, test extraction, adapters, spikes, or candidate branches when it reduces critical-path latency. Shipping broad domain behavior still requires its owning platform/contracts and release evidence to be qualified.

## 20.3 SDD contract

Every material work node defines:

- intended outcome, stated solution request where relevant, and XY/non-goal analysis;
- controlling requirement IDs;
- stakeholders/system boundary;
- capability-gap and FOSS/owner-code reuse disposition;
- interfaces and canonical ownership;
- adopted-upstream boundary, exact source identity, and replacement/update seam where applicable;
- security/privacy/rights/license/threats;
- responsive/input/accessibility behavior;
- offline/degraded/failure/Recovery behavior;
- migration/rollback;
- acceptance evidence, including characterization parity where applicable;
- maintenance/upstream-update/retirement implications.

Requirements SHOULD state observable outcome, interfaces, constraints, degradation, and evidence rather than prematurely hard-code mechanisms.

## 20.4 TDD contract

Canonical loop:

RED - the relevant executable oracle fails for the expected unmet behavior.

GREEN - the smallest implementation makes the relevant oracle pass.

REFACTOR - improve structure without changing accepted behavior.

PROVE - run all affected regression, integration, target, accessibility, security, Recovery, performance, migration, and release evidence.

Tests SHALL NOT be weakened, filtered, skipped, quarantined, threshold-relaxed, or rewritten merely to make implementation pass.

A test written after implementation may be regression evidence but SHALL NOT be falsely described as historical RED evidence.

Material defects SHALL receive durable regression guards.

When materially adopting/transplanting existing FOSS behavior, preserve or create characterization tests before semantic refactor where practical. A successful upstream test suite is evidence about upstream behavior, not proof that Omnevum integration, accessibility, security, data ownership, or target support is correct.

Critical invariants SHOULD use stronger methods where proportionate: property tests, fuzzing, mutation testing, model-based testing, deterministic replay, fault injection, and adversarial browser/agent testing.

## 20.5 Durable controller

The engineering project SHALL NOT live only in chat.

A versioned durable controller/control manifest shall track or locate:

- accepted objective/scope/revision;
- MPES/spec digests;
- requirements/SDD/ADR/threat model;
- work/dependency graph;
- repository/source state;
- tests/evidence;
- blockers/watches;
- migrations;
- upstream lineage;
- FOSS/dependency identities;
- support/currentness state;
- release/rollback state;
- pending/uncertain external effects;
- exact next action;
- engineering Recovery state.

Use existing repository/CI/coding-agent primitives where possible. Do not invent a new daemon/workflow platform merely to satisfy this responsibility.

## 20.6 STATUS / RESUME / STOP

STATUS reports exact scope/revision, verified progress, evidence/acceptance state, currentness, assumptions, blockers, pending effects, authority/resource state, artifacts, and exact next action.

RESUME reconciles actual repository/files/controller state, current external effects, qualifications, and currentness before continuing.

STOP halts controllable workers/jobs/subagents, checkpoints recoverable state, preserves rollback/Recovery, and surfaces uncertain/irreversible effects.

## 20.7 Subagents and parallelism

The AI engineer MAY spawn any number of specialist subagents/parallel workers within available capability when decomposition, specialization, latency reduction, or independent evidence creates positive leverage.

Each subagent SHOULD have a bounded mission, context, tools, resources, workspace, effects, verification obligation, fallback, and expiry.

Parallel mutable work SHOULD be isolated and reconciled before canonical integration.

Do not add multi-agent complexity when one agent is faster or safer.

## 20.8 Blocker elimination

Before escalating any blocker, proportionately attempt applicable routes:

- inspect canon/repository/history/evidence;
- current authoritative research;
- deterministic debugging/testing/profiling;
- specialist model/agent;
- mature FOSS/native substitution;
- alternate architecture/backend;
- new adapter/harness/simulator/oracle;
- decomposition;
- safe reversible assumption;
- bounded experiment;
- environment repair;
- materially different retry hypothesis.

Ordinary machine effort, latency, complexity, or inconvenience is not a reason to hand avoidable work to the human.

## 20.9 Fresh-agent resumability

A fresh qualified agent using only a clean clone, this MPES/control artifacts, normal source interfaces, and declared credentials/access SHALL be able to continue the project without original chat history.

At least one benchmark SHALL intentionally interrupt a nontrivial app/game implementation and resume with a fresh agent from durable project truth.

## 20.10 Engineering Recovery

Maintain an integrity-manifested Recovery bundle sufficient to reconstruct objective, requirements, SDD, source/toolchain identity, tests, work/evidence ledger, blockers, currentness, migrations, release state, and pending external effects without original chat or live credentials.

Restored credentials/leases remain inert until current environment/authority is re-established.

## 20.11 Engineering agent-host and subscription reuse

The durable Omnevum engineering controller, requirements, evidence, repository state, tests and release authority SHALL remain independent of the particular coding-agent host that executes work.

OMN-ENG-001: Before building an Omnevum-specific coding-agent runtime/daemon, benchmark current FOSS agent hosts/harnesses and official vendor agents on representative Omnevum work. Candidate families include OpenHands, SWE-agent/mini-SWE-agent/SWE-ReX, goose, aider, Cline, OpenCode, durable execution harnesses such as Relay, host-neutral agent adapters such as harness-class projects, qualified successors, and later entrants. Currentness SHALL remove or demote archived/read-only hosts from the active benchmark set rather than preserving them by historical popularity. The selected host MAY be replaced at any time without losing accepted engineering state.

OMN-ENG-002: Where the owner already pays for an AI subscription that officially includes a coding/agent surface, the engineering factory SHOULD prefer that zero- or lower-marginal-cost route when it passes qualification and current terms permit the automated mode. Current examples include ChatGPT-plan Codex, Claude-plan Claude Code/Agent SDK, and Google AI Pro/Ultra Antigravity; their entitlements, task scopes and automation interfaces are volatile and SHALL be currentness-gated. Provider consumer-web scraping or OAuth/session piggybacking is not a substitute for an official agent surface.

OMN-ENG-003: Agent-host qualification SHALL exercise noninteractive/structured output, actual sandbox/containment versus permission-prompt behavior, cancellation/timeouts, repository isolation, resumability, context handling, tool/command evidence, and failure reporting. A host's permission UI, read-only agent label, or provider "full auto" mode SHALL NOT be treated as OS/process containment without current evidence. A provider's own "full auto" or subscription allowance SHALL NOT bypass Omnevum's protected tests, authority tiers, release gates, or evidence integrity.

OMN-ENG-004: Multi-agent orchestration SHOULD reuse host-native subagents/worktrees/sandboxes when they satisfy Omnevum contracts, but the controller SHALL preserve enough neutral state to resume the same work under a materially different host/model when quotas, outages, terms, bugs, or quality change.

## 22.3 Reproducibility and provenance

Accepted releases SHOULD be content-reproducible from exact source/dependency/toolchain/configuration where practical.

Maintain an SBOM/dependency/license inventory proportionate to release exposure.

Where cryptographic release identity or supply-chain attestations materially improve the accepted distribution profile, the release plane SHOULD evaluate mature open tooling/standards such as Sigstore/cosign bundles and in-toto-compatible attestations before designing bespoke signature/provenance formats. The chosen scheme SHALL bind the exact artifact and relevant source/build identity, expose verification instructions, preserve offline/long-term verification material where required, and distinguish integrity/provenance from safety. Identity-bearing transparency logs can create durable public metadata; keyless/public-log use SHALL therefore be an explicit release-profile decision with privacy implications rather than a hidden default.

When supposedly reproducible artifacts differ, the engineering plane SHOULD use a qualified recursive binary/archive comparison tool such as diffoscope or an equivalent to localize nondeterminism before weakening the reproducibility claim or normalizing opaque drift.

## 22.4 Dependency reconstruction

Lockfiles/version strings alone do not prove future availability. Where legal and practical, retain license-compliant content-addressed reconstruction inputs for non-regenerable material build dependencies.

Registry/Git/CDN disappearance SHALL NOT silently substitute different content while claiming exact reconstruction.

## 22.5 GitHub Pages deployment

The accepted static artifact SHALL be deployable to GitHub Pages using a reproducible project-owned deployment path. GitHub Actions MAY automate the build/deploy but is not a runtime requirement.

A user or fork maintainer SHOULD be able to build the static distribution locally and publish it to an ordinary static HTTPS host.
## 22.6 Automated dependency/upstream maintenance

OMN-RELSE-001: The engineering repository SHOULD use a qualified dependency-update bot such as Renovate or an equivalent mechanism to surface current dependency updates with changelog/context, but generated update PRs SHALL remain candidates subject to Omnevum gates.

OMN-RELSE-002: Release/security CI SHOULD use current qualified FOSS tooling for vulnerability, SBOM, and CI-workflow analysis where it materially improves detection. Current examples include OSV-Scanner, Syft, Grype, and zizmor; exact tools are replaceable and version-bound.

OMN-RELSE-003: OpenSSF Scorecard or comparable external project posture signals MAY inform upstream triage, but popularity, score, badge, star count, or any single scanner SHALL NOT substitute for exact source review and Omnevum qualification.

OMN-RELSE-004: Automated update machinery SHALL support grouping/noise reduction, dependency dashboards or equivalent queues, immutable/pinned workflow identities where practical, and separate handling of major/breaking updates. Routine patch/minor updates MAY be automatically prepared and tested, but auto-merge is permitted only when the exact configured risk/profile gates make that behavior safe and all mandatory evidence passes.

OMN-RELSE-005: An upstream-security or dependency-update tool is itself a supply-chain dependency and SHALL be pinned/qualified/updated under the same currentness model; scanner compromise or stale vulnerability data SHALL not become a hidden trust root.

## 22.7 Automated FOSS license and source-obligation compliance

Aggressive FOSS code adoption SHALL be matched by aggressive automation of license/provenance obligations so compliance does not become the manual bottleneck that discourages reuse.

OMN-RELSE-006: The engineering/release plane SHALL evaluate mature FOSS compliance tooling such as OSS Review Toolkit (ORT), ScanCode Toolkit, REUSE/SPDX tooling, FOSSology, and qualified successors before building bespoke license scanners/report generators. The accepted pipeline SHOULD automate dependency/source inventory, license/copyright findings, policy evaluation, notices/attribution, SPDX/CycloneDX outputs, corresponding/source-archive reconstruction where required, and review queues for ambiguous findings.

Tool output is evidence, not legal truth. Ambiguous compatibility or distribution obligations that materially affect release licensing remain an external legal/rights gate, but the engineering system SHALL prepare the smallest precise question plus complete provenance/source evidence rather than escalating ordinary detectable obligations to the human.

- buildable;
- testable;
- packageable;
- upgradable;
- rollback-capable where data compatibility permits;
- disableable;
- uninstallable;
